# Comparing Knowledge3D with other approaches

**Status: superseded.**
The February 2026 comparison has been withdrawn as an assessment of relative capability.
Its rankings by years ahead, superiority claims and interpretations of people's comments were not supported by matched public evaluations.
Use the [explainer](EXPLAINER.md) for the current proposal and its alternatives.

## Questions retained for research

The earlier paper compared where a system stores knowledge, how it composes procedures, what a person can inspect, and how it controls access.
Those remain useful comparison questions.
A comparison needs the same task, quality criteria, workload, hardware assumptions, data and measurement method for each system.
A design difference does not establish better accuracy, lower cost, security or a lead measured in years.
Questions and expressions of interest from other people must be reported as those statements, with their public context, rather than translated into validation.

## Evidence

The [evidence matrix](PM_KR_EVIDENCE_VALIDATION_MATRIX.md) describes what a result would need to establish.
Daniel's [28 October 2026 status](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-pm-kr/2026Oct/0016.html) states that code work is paused while specifications and group deliverables are the focus.
The project makes no ranking claim here.
