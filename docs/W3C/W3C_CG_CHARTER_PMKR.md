# PM-KR: historical charter proposal

**Status: superseded.**
This is a record of Knowledge3D's February 2026 charter proposal, not the current governance of the PM-KR Community Group.
The proposed dates, staffing, voting rules, certification plans and industry-adoption targets were not evidence of adopted group decisions.
The [group's public page](https://www.w3.org/community/pm-kr/) and [group repository](https://github.com/w3c-cg/pm-kr) are the places to follow its work.

## Scope proposed

The draft proposed studying reusable procedures, shared references, provenance, human and AI access, and exchange with existing knowledge representations.
It suggested a data model, conformance profiles, interoperability mappings and tests as possible deliverables.
These suggestions inform discussion; the group determines its own work.
Knowledge3D is one project contributing ideas and does not constrain participation or other implementations.
See the [explainer](EXPLAINER.md) and [document index](README.md) for the current project inputs.
