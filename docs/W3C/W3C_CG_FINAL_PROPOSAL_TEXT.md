# PM-KR: historical group proposal text

**Status: superseded.**
The February 2026 draft in this file prepared wording for a Community Group proposal.
Its sending instructions and predicted launch schedule have been retired.
The [PM-KR Community Group page](https://www.w3.org/community/pm-kr/) now carries the group's public description.

## Proposal retained in summary

The proposal asks whether knowledge represented by reusable procedures and shared references can reduce maintenance of overlapping representations.
It considers people and AI accessing the same underlying knowledge, with presentations suited to each reader.
The group's stated areas of study include data models, execution semantics, conformance and relationships with RDF, OWL and JSON-LD.
Knowledge3D may inform those discussions, while the group remains open to other approaches and implementations.
