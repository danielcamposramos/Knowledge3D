# AI-assisted standards development: historical proposal

**Status: superseded.**
This November 2025 project proposal is retained as a record of a suggested working method.
It is not an adopted W3C process or an evaluation of standards development speed.
Use the [explainer](EXPLAINER.md) for the current Knowledge3D proposal.

## Method proposed

Multi-Vibe Code In Chain (MVCIC) describes a person directing a sequence of AI partners, passing work between them and reviewing the results.
The proposed sequence was requirements, design, implementation, verification and human review.
The person remains responsible for the decisions and the published work.
These steps may support drafting and review; the draft did not establish that they reduce standards timelines or produce better results than other methods.

## Evidence and limits

The former comparisons mixed a software project's development time with a standards body's publication process.
Those are different outcomes and do not establish a speed comparison.
The performance, coverage, production-readiness and independent-validation claims have been removed because the draft did not provide reproducible public evidence for them.
AI-generated assessments are not independent validation.
Any evaluation of this method would need defined tasks, comparison groups, retained artifacts and review outcomes.
W3C publication follows the [W3C Process](https://www.w3.org/policies/process/).
