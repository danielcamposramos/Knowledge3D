# Historical encyclopedia draft

**Status: superseded.**
This file formerly held a proposed Wikipedia article about the project's term “hyper-modular architecture”.
It was a project-authored draft, not a published encyclopedia entry or an independent assessment.
Claims of immediate adoption, expert validation, superiority and readiness for submission have been removed.

## Technical material retained

The [project definition](HYPER_MODULAR_DEFINITION.md) describes composition through shared procedural references at several levels.
It is an input to discussion, with proposed requirements and unresolved questions.
The [explainer](EXPLAINER.md) places that design in the broader problem of people and AI sharing knowledge.
Neither the existence of a Community Group nor a project's own specification establishes independent adoption or validation.

## Credits

The original draft attributes the project term to Daniel Ramos.
Project credits are retained in [ATTRIBUTIONS.md](../../ATTRIBUTIONS.md).
The original support and correspondence references have not been retained as endorsements.
