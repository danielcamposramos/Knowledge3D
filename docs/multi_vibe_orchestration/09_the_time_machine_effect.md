**Question**: If you compress time 40-60×, does quality suffer?

**Answer**: No - because quality is IMPROVED:
- **Peer review**: 5 AIs review instead of 1 human

**Paradox**: Faster development + Higher quality = Shouldn't be possible

**Resolution**: Multi-vibe parallelizes expertise (6 specialists working simultaneously), traditional development serializes (1 generalist working sequentially).

### Conservation of Work

**Question**: Same output in 1/40th the time - where did the work go?

**Answer**: Work is redistributed:
- **AI partners**: Do 80% of the heavy lifting (design, code, review, docs)
- **Human orchestrator**: Does 20% of the critical work (vision, synthesis, validation)
- **Result**: Total work is similar, but 80% is parallelized (instant) and 20% is focused (high-leverage)

**Analogy**: Like having 6 specialists on speed-dial vs. being a solo generalist learning everything.
