## 🎯 Success Metrics

**Good weekly summary:**
- ✅ Concise (~150 lines, no AI verbosity)
- ✅ Factual (progress numbers, concrete milestones)
- ✅ Actionable (clear next steps)
- ✅ Acknowledges community (feedback/contributors mentioned)
- ✅ Cross-CG aware (references AIKR/CogAI/WebML activity)

**Bad weekly summary:**
- ❌ Verbose (500+ lines, AI chatbot style)
- ❌ Vague ("good progress was made...")
- ❌ No community acknowledgment
- ❌ Isolated (doesn't mention cross-CG collaboration)
