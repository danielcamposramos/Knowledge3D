# Daniel Campos Ramos Bio

[INSERT: Daniel headshot, professional, 300x300px]

Daniel Campos Ramos is an electrical engineer and founder of EchoSystems AI Studios in Brasilia, Brazil. He is co-chair of the W3C PM-KR Community Group and lead architect of Knowledge3D (K3D), the reference implementation used to test procedural memory standards under real hardware constraints.

His work combines PTX-first systems engineering, spatial user interface design, and procedural knowledge representation. In K3D, he has led sovereign runtime implementation, including dozens of hand-written GPU kernels and strict hot-path dependency controls. His engineering approach emphasizes deterministic traces, measurable latency behavior, and inspectable reasoning flows.

Daniel also introduced the Multi-Vibe Code In Chain (MVCIC) collaboration method for coordinated human and AI development cycles, with architecture, implementation, and validation loops grounded in repository artifacts. He focuses on practical standards adoption: how to move from concept papers to reproducible tooling that W3C members, developers, and institutions can evaluate directly.

From Brazil, he is advancing a MERCOSUR-EU collaboration track with co-chair Milton Ponson to align technical rigor, sustainability outcomes, and standards governance for next-generation AI and web infrastructure.
He maintains a strong implementation-first culture where claims are tied to tests, logs, and documented reproduction steps.
