# Christoph Dorn Bio

[INSERT: Christoph headshot, professional, 300x300px]

Christoph Dorn is a sovereignty and boundary systems architect based in Canada and founder of Stream44.Studio. He contributes governance architecture to PM-KR and Knowledge3D through explicit boundary logic, policy contracts, and privacy-transparency balancing.

His guidance influenced the project's boundary-contract direction: hard boundaries, soft boundaries, and ambiguity boundaries encoded as inspectable rules rather than informal policy text. He has stressed that radical transparency is not always safe, and that hidden structures are sometimes necessary when paired with accountable audit pathways.

For PM-KR mission work, his contributions include governance framing for deterministic traces, authority escalation points, and role-based disclosure levels. This work aligns with practical deployment concerns in enterprise, public-interest, and regulated contexts where both privacy and verifiability are required.

In the related sovereignty charter mission, the team processed and synthesized a large corpus and translated findings into implementation recommendations for PM-KR contracts and runtime decision gates. His perspective helps connect philosophical sovereignty principles to executable technical standards.
His contribution is especially valuable where systems must balance operational privacy, legal accountability, and practical transparency without collapsing into simplistic policy binaries.
He emphasizes executable governance over abstract policy slogans.
