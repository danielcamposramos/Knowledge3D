# Use Case: Sustainable AI

## Target Audience
Sustainable Web IG participants, environmental research groups, carbon-aware AI operators.

## Core Hook
A standards-driven reduction of duplicated knowledge and repeated compute can materially lower AI energy demand over time.

## Headline Scenario
The K3D carbon blueprint models a 10-year cumulative savings scenario of 12 Gt CO2, with annual savings reaching 2.5 Gt by 2035 under large-scale adoption assumptions.

## Mechanism
1. Compression and deduplication
- Canonical procedural source plus references reduces repeated payload storage.

2. Reuse over retraining/reprocessing
- Procedural reuse can reduce repetitive generation and transformation overhead.

3. Bounded runtime paths
- Explicit runtime contracts reduce hidden compute expansion in production paths.

## Context
The carbon model compares projected savings against major industrial emission frames and positions procedural memory as an architecture response to AI energy growth.

## Required Framing for Media
- This is a modeled scenario, not a guaranteed baseline.
- Assumptions and methods are documented and should be reviewed with the source model.
- Future iterations should refine projections with deployment evidence.

## Why This Use Case Is Strong
Sustainable AI often focuses on incremental optimization. PM-KR contributes a structural strategy: reduce duplicated knowledge work at the representation level.
