# Carbon Comparison Context

## Primary Scenario Values
- 12.0 Gt CO2 cumulative over 10 years (modeled)
- 2.5 Gt CO2 per year by 2035 (modeled)

## Industry Context Frames
Use these as communication context labels, not absolute equivalence claims:

1. Aviation-scale framing
A 12 Gt cumulative reduction over a decade can be communicated as roughly "multiple years of global aviation-scale emissions," depending on baseline source used.

2. Shipping-scale framing
A 2.5 Gt annual reduction frame can be communicated as "comparable to major global transport-sector emissions orders of magnitude."

3. Global-share framing
The blueprint uses a 6.9% global emissions context for 2035 scenario discussion.

## Media-Safe Wording
Preferred:
- "modeled equivalent"
- "order-of-magnitude comparison"
- "scenario context"

Avoid:
- "exactly equal to"
- "guaranteed reduction"
- "fixed annual outcome"

## Recommended Public Sentence
"Under the published PM-KR scenario assumptions, projected reductions are large enough to be discussed in the same order of magnitude as major transport-sector emissions, with cumulative impact modeled at 12 Gt over 10 years."
