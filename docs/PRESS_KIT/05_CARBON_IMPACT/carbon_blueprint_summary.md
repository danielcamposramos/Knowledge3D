# Carbon Blueprint Summary (Executive)

## Headline
PM-KR and K3D publish a modeled 10-year climate impact scenario with cumulative savings of 12 Gt CO2 and annual savings reaching 2.5 Gt/year by 2035 under broad procedural adoption assumptions.

## Why This Is Material
The model frames architecture-level savings, not only hardware or scheduling optimization. It focuses on reducing duplicated knowledge work, repeated transforms, and avoidable compute overhead.

## Key Figures Used in Press Materials
- 10-year cumulative modeled savings: 12.0 Gt CO2
- Annual modeled savings by 2035: 2.5 Gt CO2/year
- Context framing: 6.9% of global emissions (scenario context)

## Scope Reminder
These values are projection outputs from documented assumptions. They are not guaranteed deployment baselines. They should be communicated with methodological context.

## Communication Guidance
Use three-part framing in interviews:
1. Problem: AI and digital systems duplicate knowledge and compute.
2. Mechanism: canonical procedural memory plus references reduces duplication and repeated processing.
3. Evidence posture: model today, validate continuously with implementation data.
