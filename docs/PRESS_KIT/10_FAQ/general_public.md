# General Public FAQ

## 1. What is procedural memory?
A way to store knowledge once as canonical procedures and reuse it through references.

## 2. How is this different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT-style systems focus on model-generated responses; PM-KR focuses on shared canonical knowledge structures with auditable execution paths.

## 3. Why does this reduce carbon emissions?
Reducing duplicated data and repeated compute can lower total energy demand over time.

## 4. What is the W3C?
The World Wide Web Consortium is the standards body founded by Tim Berners-Lee for web standards.

## 5. Who is Tim Berners-Lee?
Inventor of the World Wide Web and founder of W3C.

## 6. What does MERCOSUR-EU collaboration mean here?
A technical partnership narrative linking Brazil and Netherlands leadership in standards and implementation work.

## 7. When will this reach web browsers?
Roadmap discussions target phased standards and prototype integration tracks through 2026-2027.

## 8. Is this open source?
Yes, Knowledge3D is open-source and used as a reference implementation.

## 9. Can I try it now?
You can review the repository, videos, and published docs; hands-on setup depends on your environment.

## 10. How much carbon does AI produce today?
Large and rising; PM-KR provides an architecture response, with published scenario modeling.

## 11. What is Knowledge3D?
A procedural memory and spatial knowledge reference implementation aligned with PM-KR goals.

## 12. Who are Daniel and Milton?
Co-chairs of PM-KR: Daniel Campos Ramos (Brazil) and Milton Ponson (Netherlands).

## 13. Why Brazil and Netherlands?
It represents a practical MERCOSUR-EU standards collaboration in frontier technology.

## 14. How does this help accessibility?
One canonical source can drive multiple outputs, including visual and assistive pathways.
