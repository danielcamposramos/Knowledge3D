# SGI Term Origin: Proof of Canonical Definition

**Date**: March 4, 2026

## Research Question

**Before March 4, 2026**: Was "Spatial General Intelligence (SGI)" a clearly defined term in AI research?

**Answer**: **NO.** The term "SGI" had multiple conflicting meanings, and "Spatial General Intelligence" did not exist as a coherent paradigm.

---

## What "SGI" Meant BEFORE Our Definition (March 4, 2026)

### 1. **Specialized Generalist Intelligence (SGI)** ⭐ Most Common
- **Definition**: AI that specializes in at least one task (surpassing human experts) while retaining general abilities
- **Source**: [ArXiv Paper (July 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08642v1) - "Towards Building Specialized Generalist AI with System 1 and System 2 Fusion"
- **Quote**: "SGI is defined as AI that specializes in at least one task, surpassing human experts, while also retaining general abilities."
- **Problem**: Focused on task specialization (NOT spatial grounding)

---

### 2. **Scientific General Intelligence (SGI)**
- **Definition**: AI that autonomously navigates the full cycle of scientific inquiry (Deliberation, Conception, Action, Perception)
- **Source**: [Emergent Mind - Scientific General Intelligence](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/scientific-general-intelligence-sgi)
- **Quote**: "SGI denotes an AI system that can autonomously navigate the full, iterative cycle of scientific inquiry."
- **Problem**: Domain-specific (science), NOT general spatial paradigm

---

### 3. **Superhuman General Intelligence (SGI)**
- **Definition**: Intelligence surpassing human capability across all domains
- **Source**: LinkedIn discussions (speculative)
- **Quote**: "Superhuman General Intelligence (SGI) will make breakthrough discoveries in physics with zero point energy and antigravity."
- **Problem**: Futurist speculation (not grounded paradigm)

---

### 4. **Symbiotic General Intelligence (SGI)**
- **Definition**: Human-AI co-evolving network whose intelligence emerges through interaction
- **Source**: [Medium Article](https://medium.com/@tmcrg85/the-sgi-paradigm-rethinking-the-pursuit-of-artificial-general-intelligence-1f368e830d4f)
- **Quote**: "Symbiotic General Intelligence (SGI), which is a system—a recursive, co-evolving network of human and AI nodes."
- **Problem**: Focused on interaction (not spatial environment)

---

### 5. **Smart Grid and Artificial Intelligence (SGAI/SGI)**
- **Definition**: Conference series on smart grids + AI integration
- **Source**: [2026 3rd International Conference on Smart Grid and Artificial Intelligence](https://www.icsgai.com/SGAI2025)
- **Problem**: Domain-specific (energy grids), NOT general AI paradigm

---

### 6. **BCS SGAI (Specialist Group on Artificial Intelligence)**
- **Definition**: British Computer Society's AI specialist group
- **Source**: [BCS SGAI AI-2026 Conference](https://www.bcs-sgai.org/ai2026/)
- **Problem**: Organizational acronym (not AI paradigm)

## What "Spatial Intelligence" Meant (NOT "Spatial General Intelligence")

### Fei-Fei Li's "Spatial Intelligence" (2025-2026)
- **Definition**: AI's ability to perceive, reason about, and interact with the 3D world
- **Source**: [TIME - Spatial Intelligence Is AI's Next Frontier](https://time.com/7339693/fei-fei-li-ai/)
- **Quote**: "Spatial intelligence is the scaffolding upon which our cognition is built... Building spatially intelligent AI requires world models."
- **Key Limitation**: Focused on 3D perception/navigation (NOT "General Intelligence" + NOT shared human-AI environment)

**What's Missing**:
- No concept of "General Intelligence" (spatial ≠ general)
- No shared human-AI workspace (AI builds world models alone)
- No dual-client transparency (AI's 3D understanding ≠ human's view)

---

### Robotics/Autonomous Vehicles "Spatial AI"
- **Definition**: Navigation, manipulation, object recognition in 3D
- **Sources**:
  - [GeeksforGeeks - Spatial Intelligence in AI](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/artificial-intelligence/spatial-intelligence-in-ai/)
  - [NVIDIA - Building Spatial Intelligence](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/building-spatial-intelligence-from-real-world-3d-data-using-deep-learning-framework-fvdb)
- **Key Limitation**: Narrow tasks (navigation, object detection) — NOT general intelligence

---

### Psychology "Spatial Intelligence" (Gardner's Multiple Intelligences)
- **Definition**: Visual-spatial reasoning ability in humans
- **Source**: [Wikipedia - Spatial Intelligence (Psychology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_intelligence_(psychology))
- **Key Limitation**: Human cognitive ability (NOT AI paradigm)

**Spatial General Intelligence (SGI)**: The paradigm where intelligence—both human and artificial—operates within a shared, navigable 3D spatial environment. Unlike AGI (abstract, opaque), SGI grounds intelligence in spatial reality where humans and AI cohabit the same cognitive workspace.

**5 Core Principles** (DEFINED BY US):
1. **Spatial Grounding**: Intelligence operates in navigable 3D (Galaxy Universe)

## Philosophical Analysis: Why The Gap Existed

### 1. **Spatial Intelligence** (Fei-Fei Li, World Labs) — NOT SGI
**Focus**: AI's 3D perception (world models, robotics, autonomous vehicles)
**Missing**: General intelligence + shared human-AI environment + dual-client transparency

**Quote** (Fei-Fei Li, TIME 2026):
> "Spatial intelligence is the scaffolding upon which our cognition is built... Building spatially intelligent AI requires world models."

**Problem**: AI builds SEPARATE world models (not shared with humans). No concept of dual-client contract.

---

### 2. **AGI** (OpenAI, DeepMind) — NOT SGI
**Focus**: Abstract reasoning (LLMs, parameter scaling)
**Missing**: Spatial grounding + transparency + shared environment

**Quote** (AWS AGI Definition):
> "Artificial general intelligence (AGI) is the ability of AI systems to think and act like humans."

**Problem**: AGI is opaque (black-box neural nets), abstract (no spatial grounding), separate (AI embeddings ≠ human visuals).

---

### 3. **Specialized Generalist Intelligence** (ArXiv 2024) — NOT SGI
**Focus**: Task specialization + general abilities (System 1 + System 2 fusion)
**Missing**: Spatial environment + human-AI shared reality

**Quote** (ArXiv):
> "SGI is defined as AI that specializes in at least one task, surpassing human experts, while also retaining general abilities."

**Problem**: Still abstract reasoning (not spatially grounded). No shared workspace.

---

### 4. **Symbiotic General Intelligence** (Medium) — Closest, But Still NOT SGI
**Focus**: Human-AI co-evolution through interaction
**Missing**: Spatial substrate + procedural composition + web-native standards

**Quote** (Medium):
> "Symbiotic General Intelligence (SGI), which is a system—a recursive, co-evolving network of human and AI nodes whose intelligence emerges through interaction, not isolation."

**Closest Concept**: Recognizes human-AI collaboration, BUT:
- No spatial grounding (abstract network, not 3D environment)
