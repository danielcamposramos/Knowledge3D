## Summary
DIGITIZATION: THE ERASURE OF ANALOG SOVEREIGNTY Digitization is always sold as convenience — instant access, frictionless transfers, global reach. Digitization allows: Custody without possession Ownership without title Assets without physical form Transactions without presence Promises without accountability Control without visibility Once wealth becomes data, it becomes: Movable Confiscable Freezable Rehypothecable Reclassifiable Deletable All at the discretion of the entity controlling the ledger. It guarantees the privileged receive priority automatically In a digital cascade: The largest secured creditors are satisfied first Their collateral claims are filled instantly The system prioritizes them algorithmically Losses are pushed down the chain so fast there is no opportunity to interrupt the flow Speed is the mechanism by which the rich become richer during collapse.
