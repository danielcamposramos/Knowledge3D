## Summary
The Secret Team — Operations and bypass mechanisms If Quigley explains who coordinates , Prouty explains how coordination is executed without consent or oversight . As a former Air Force officer and CIA liaison to the Pentagon, Prouty describes the emergence of parallel command structures — covert policy channels that bypass constitutional authority while maintaining plausible deniability. Here, power no longer appears as debate or legislation, but as execution : operations without declarations wars without votes outcomes without accountability This is where the abstraction of “the state” collapses into something far more precise: parallel governance , operating alongside — and often above — the visible one.
