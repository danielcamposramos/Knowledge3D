## Summary
Smuggling in authority under the guise of decentralization Blockchain is often sold as: “Trustless” In practice: you still trust oracles developers governance structures miners / validators protocol maintainers upgrade authorities Trust is not eliminated. Assigning it ethical or sovereign properties Claims like: “Blockchain enforces fairness” “Blockchain enables self-sovereign identity” “Blockchain decentralizes power” “Blockchain prevents abuse” These are non-sequitur claims . Why the hype persists Because blockchain is: legible to capital legible to regulators legible to automation legible to AI legible to surveillance legible to control systems And illegible to lived human nuance .

## Key Sentences
- Smuggling in authority under the guise of decentralization Blockchain is often sold as: “Trustless” In practice: you still trust oracles developers governance structures miners / validators protocol maintainers upgrade authorities Trust is not eliminated.
- Assigning it ethical or sovereign properties Claims like: “Blockchain enforces fairness” “Blockchain enables self-sovereign identity” “Blockchain decentralizes power” “Blockchain prevents abuse” These are non-sequitur claims .
- Why the hype persists Because blockchain is: legible to capital legible to regulators legible to automation legible to AI legible to surveillance legible to control systems And illegible to lived human nuance .
- It is: coherent, but not complete precise, but not wise immutable, but not true The uncomfortable conclusion Blockchain is excellent for: narrow accounting settlement under agreed rules timestamped records constrained environments It is catastrophic when presented as: a substitute for judgment a replacement for trust a guarantor of truth a foundation for human sovereignty Used honestly, it’s a tool.
- Conclusion Most blockchain discourse: projects human qualities onto machines replaces responsibility with procedure substitutes ethics with math confuses permanence with legitimacy That is a civilizational error we have found re: AI, finance, biotech, governance…..
