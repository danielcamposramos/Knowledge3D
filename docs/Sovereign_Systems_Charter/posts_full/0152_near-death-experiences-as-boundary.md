## Summary
Near-Death Experiences as Boundary Evidence harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Near-Death Experiences as Boundary Evidence What They Reveal Without Proving Anything harry blazer Dec 30, 2025 2 1 Share Near-death experiences are routinely framed as a binary dispute. Boundary evidence does not refute a system It reveals its limits In every mature domain of knowledge, there are phenomena that do not overthrow the system, yet cannot be fully contained by it. Why NDEs are not well explained as “just hallucinations” Hallucinations caused by drugs, delirium, or neurological injury tend to have certain properties: Fragmentation Ego inflation or paranoia Loss of narrative continuity Rapid decay with minimal long-term integration Near-death experiences reliably show the opposite pattern: Heightened clarity Narrative coherence Ethical integration Permanent personality transformation This inversion matters.
