## Key Sentences
- What it has become far less adept at measuring is coherence — the subtle alignment of timing, rhythm and resonance that allows complex living systems to remain stable under load.
- Why the toxicity model keeps failing Most environmental health questions are framed through a toxicological lens.
- Healthy systems display: Rhythmic variability Phase flexibility Recovery intervals Redundancy Noise tolerance These traits allow organisms to remain stable in fluctuating environments.
- Symptoms may include: Reduced resilience to stress Poor recovery Dysregulation across multiple systems Heightened sensitivity to secondary insults Developmental vulnerability These outcomes do not map cleanly onto single causes or single exposures.
- Developing systems are: More plastic More sensitive to entrainment Less buffered by redundancy Still establishing baseline rhythms Subtle environmental forcing during these windows can shape lifelong regulation without ever producing acute injury.
