- After reaffirming sovereign self-defense and rejecting the fantasy of making autonomous systems “moral,” we arrive at the unavoidable conclusion: Some machines are incompatible with human sovereignty, regardless of safeguards, intentions or claimed necessity.
- Any system that can apply force, restraint, pursuit or compulsion to a human being without that person’s contemporaneous consent and without accountable human agency is incompatible with sovereignty.
- Prohibited class one: autonomous coercive agents in civil space These systems should never exist outside tightly controlled military contexts, if at all: autonomous patrol robots autonomous law enforcement robots autonomous crowd control systems autonomous pursuit or apprehension machines autonomous weapons deployed among civilians This prohibition applies by capability , not by mission statement.
- The final test of legitimacy Here is the test every proposed embodied system must pass: If this system were used against you, in error, without recourse, would sovereignty survive?
