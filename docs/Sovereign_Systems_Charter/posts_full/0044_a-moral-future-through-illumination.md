## Summary
When systems failed, they failed loudly — through war, collapse or revolt — and the moral lessons, however costly, were legible. From moral failure to moral erosion The dominant moral failure of our time is not malice. Moral erosion occurs when: decisions are optimized rather than judged responsibility is diffused into systems intent substitutes for consequence speed replaces pause legality replaces legitimacy No one declares themselves immoral.
