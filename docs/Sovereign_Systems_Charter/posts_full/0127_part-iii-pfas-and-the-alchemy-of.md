## Summary
From Debugging Code to Debugging Reality By the time the IT industry normalized the idea that: products ship unfinished users encounter failures fixes arrive later (if at all) it had quietly inverted a core assumption of civilization: Prototyping used to precede deployment. PFAS: Chemistry Designed to Never Fail PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) were engineered to be: Thermally stable Chemically inert Hydrophobic and oleophobic Resistant to degradation From an engineering standpoint, they were a triumph. Water systems became test rigs Human blood became assay medium Wildlife became longitudinal data Time itself became the experiment This is customer-subsidized debugging , scaled to society.
