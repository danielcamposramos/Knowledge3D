- Your entire essay rests on: Clear boundaries Correspondence to reality Conjecture and refutation Protection against noise If we introduce PSI as a knowledge category without strong empirical grounding, we blur the boundary between disciplined epistemology and narrative comfort — which is exactly what you’ve been warning against.
- The Important Boundary If sovereignty depends on: Clear epistemic standards Disciplined conjecture and refutation Refusal to subordinate truth to outcome Then sovereignty collapses the moment we treat unverified perception as equivalent to demonstrable reality.
- The Core Boundary If your sovereignty architecture is built on: Clear boundaries, Voluntary constraint, Conjecture and refutation, Protection from noise, Then the same discipline must apply to extraordinary claims.
- If you tether sovereignty to moral agency, conscience and boundary-setting, it remains defensible even under strict materialism.
- The Investigative Architecture We’re After We want a framework that satisfies four requirements at once: Openness : does not pre-dismiss phenomena because it violates reigning ontology Rigor : does not accept claims because they are compelling or identity-confirming Anti-capture : cannot be controlled by institutions, profit incentives, or narrative managers Anti-myth : cannot drift into self-sealing belief systems The architecture must be resilient against both: institutional bias and suppression human bias and self-deception That symmetry is the whole point.
