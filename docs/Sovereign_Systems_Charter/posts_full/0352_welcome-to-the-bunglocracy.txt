Subscribe Ladies and gentlemen, clowns and commanders, Welcome to the Bunglocracy , Where incompetence isn’t a bug — it’s the whole damn system. Here in the great halls of Boobocracy , We don’t govern — we gesture. We don’t lead — we livestream. And policy? That’s just buffoolery in a three-piece suit. Our elected boobocrats speak fluent bozonics — A language where sound replaces meaning, And every sentence ends with applause, confusion, or both. We have mastered the sacred art of braggarty , Where failure is disguised as bold vision, And success is measured in retweets, not results. In this brave new land, The wise are silenced, The fools are funded, And the bunglords of bureaucracy Hand out medals for mediocrity. Here, blunder is not punished. It is promoted. We don’t fear collapse — We brand it . Sell it on T-shirts. Turn it into a reality show: “America’s Got Blunders.” Our national pastime? Blunderlust. We chase catastrophe like a cat chases lasers — Eyes wide, brains off, Running in every direction but forward. This is the kingdom of booboticism , Where the robots we built to replace ourselves Now look up at us and say, &quot;Really? This is the species that made us?&quot; We have become the jesters of our own court, Laughing while the empire crumbles, Streaming our self-destruction in high-definition. So yes — Raise a toast to the Bunglocracy . Salute the Boobocracy . And remember: In a world run by fools, The wise are dangerous. harry’s Substack is a reader-supported publication.
