Nolan is a British-American immunologist, academic, inventor and business executive. He holds the Rachford and Carlota A. Harris Professor Endowed Chair in the Department of Pathology at Stanford University School of Medicine. Not a Flake. And what he says must be worthy of our consideration since he is approaching the subject with rigor and the White House at one point called him directly to tell him to shut up. His primary conclusions at the end are: There is something non-human here and it has been here for a long time, likely longer than humans have been civilized. Not sure what it is. The data shows that at the least, it is worth investigating - which he is doing. Earth is probably someone else’s property. If true, especially the last point, this would explain a lot.
