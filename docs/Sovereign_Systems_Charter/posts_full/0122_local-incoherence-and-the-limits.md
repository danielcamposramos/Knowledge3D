## Summary

Only a sovereign human being can: act under uncertainty bear responsibility without total explanation live truthfully when coherence is lived rather than recorded (explained further in footnote*) This is not sentimentality. Examples: A moral stance held consistently across changing circumstances without being reducible to a rule Integrity expressed through choice, not explanation Wisdom that shows itself in timing, restraint, or refusal, not in argument This coherence is real , but it is not always legible.
