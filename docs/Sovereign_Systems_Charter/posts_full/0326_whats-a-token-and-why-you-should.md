## Summary
In this new paradigm, the token has emerged as the fundamental unit of interaction across all digital systems: language, finance, law, identity, and even biology. The Transactability Principle Tokenization doesn’t just allow for representation — it enables agency , control , and intervention . It provides the mechanism by which: Biological processes are digitized (e.g., mRNA encoding, CRISPR editing) Cognition is interfaced (e.g., brain-computer interfaces) Social behavior is engineered (e.g., nudging algorithms, social credit systems) Reality is virtualized (e.g., the Metaverse, synthetic environments) To digitize is to decompose.
