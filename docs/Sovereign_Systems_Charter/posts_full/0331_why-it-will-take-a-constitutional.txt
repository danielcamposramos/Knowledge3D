In reality, our electoral system has been rigged — not by ballots stuffed in the night or voting machines hacked from abroad — but by a far more brazen, domestic fraud: The legal privileging of money as speech and corporations as people. The Catastrophic Role of Citizens United The Supreme Court's 2010 decision in Citizens United v. FEC marked the constitutional tipping point. In a 5–4 ruling, the Court declared that: Corporations have the same First Amendment rights as individuals , and therefore cannot be restricted in their &quot;independent political expenditures.&quot; This single vote dismantled a century of campaign finance reform. Overnight, the floodgates opened to Super PACs and dark money — channels through which billionaires, multinational corporations, and shadowy interests can spend unlimited funds to shape elections, drown out citizen voices, and manipulate public perception through targeted propaganda. This was not an act of democracy. It was an act of oligarchy — judicially sanctioned. III. Who Voted for Corporate Rule?
