The Sovereign Gospel: Reclaiming Jesus From the Victim–Absolution Machine harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in The Sovereign Gospel: Reclaiming Jesus From the Victim–Absolution Machine A Continuation Of Themes Explored Previously harry blazer Nov 18, 2025 Share There are two versions of Christianity. The first is the one Jesus actually lived and taught — a path of sovereignty, alignment, intention and responsibility. The second is the institutional version — a path of dependency, guilt, externalized evil and outsourced morality, built and maintained because it is profitable for those who provide the “service.” Most people never learn the difference. That difference is everything. 1. Jesus: A Teacher of Sovereignty, Not Subservience The Jesus of the earliest sources does not rescue people from themselves — he summons them to themselves. He calls people to: take responsibility for intention (“murder in the heart”) take responsibility for action (“by their fruits you will know them”) take responsibility for alignment (“repent” = change your aim) take responsibility for truth (“let your yes be yes”) take responsibility for sovereignty (“the kingdom is within you”) He never teaches helplessness. He refuses to let anyone hide behind tribe, doctrine, ritual, or victimhood. He treats the human being as capable of choosing alignment with the Light. That is sovereignty. 2. Sin Was Never Corruption — It Was Misalignment Before the theologians, “sin” meant something simple: Missing the mark. Straying from the path. Aiming away from the Light. No inherited stain. No metaphysical disease. No “born broken.” You were — and are — aimed wrong , not made wrong . Repentance, then, is not groveling. It is realignment : seeing clearly owning your choices adjusting your aim moving toward the Light with intention This is moral adulthood. This is sovereignty in action. 3. Evil Is Not a Creature — It Is Inversion You do not need a horned being whispering in your ear. You need only the oldest psychological pattern: The hiding from truth. The externalizing of blame. The surrender of agency. The seduction of control. The inversion of values. Every tradition understood this: “the devil” is the shape that inversion takes, both in individuals and in institutions. Whenever we replace: truth with narrative responsibility with excuses intention with obedience sovereignty with hierarchy inner Light with external authority …we have stepped into inversion. In that sense, “the devil” is not supernatural. He is structural. And he manifests wherever sovereignty dies. 4. How Sovereignty Reinterprets the Doctrines You Were Told to Obey Sin Institutional: You’re inherently corrupt. Sovereign: You’re misaligned — and capable of re-aiming. Salvation Institutional: A ticket to heaven granted by belief. Sovereign: The ongoing liberation into alignment, integrity and Light. Atonement Institutional: Someone else takes the consequences for you. Sovereign: No one can live your alignment for you. The cross reveals the cost of truth — not the elimination of responsibility. Faith Institutional: Belief in doctrines. Sovereign: Embodied trust expressed through action. Grace Institutional: A divine loophole that overrides your choices. Sovereign: Support that meets you when you move toward alignment. Repentance Institutional: Confession and reset — a spiritual mulligan. Sovereign: A deep reorientation of intention, behavior and trajectory. The Kingdom of God Institutional: A future utopia or an afterlife reward. Sovereign: A quality of reality that appears whenever humans live in truth. The Holy Spirit Institutional: A supernatural commodity dispensed by clergy. Sovereign: The inner movement toward honesty, courage, clarity and Light. Everything shifts when you remove the victim frame. Everything returns to Jesus when you remove the hierarchy. 5. Modern Institutions Run the Same Victim/Absolution Blueprint The Church was just the prototype. Political, corporate, medical, ideological and technological institutions now run the same loop: Tell you you’re powerless. Give you an external enemy to blame. Offer a branded solution that requires no inner work. Convert you into a dependent client. In religion: “You’re born sinful; we have salvation.” In politics: “You’re oppressed; we have your liberation.” In consumer culture: “You’re inadequate; we have belonging and identity for sale.” In therapy-as-industry: “You’re a diagnosis; we have permanent treatments.” In media: “You’re in danger; we have fear and outrage to keep you safe.” All share the same logic: Outsource sovereignty. Outsource responsibility. Outsource meaning. Outsource moral effort. It is always profitable for those selling the absolution. And it is always catastrophic for the human soul. 6. The Way of the Sovereign Human The sovereign path is the opposite of the victim–absolution machine. It says: You are a participant, not a pawn. You are responsible, not corrupt. You choose your alignment. You face what is true. You stand with intention. You own your actions. You seek Light directly, not through intermediaries. This is not arrogance. This is adulthood — spiritual, moral and psychological. This is the Jesus who walked the earth before the empire built its scaffolding around him. This is the gospel that cannot be monetized, institutionalized or weaponized. This is the gospel that makes human beings dangerous to every system built on fear. And this is the gospel worth recovering. Share Previous Next Discussion about this post Comments Restacks Top Latest Discussions No posts Ready for more?
