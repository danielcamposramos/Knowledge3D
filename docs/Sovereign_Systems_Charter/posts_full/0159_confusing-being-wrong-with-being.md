- The Civilizational Cost A society that punishes error and exempts implication produces: Defensiveness Dishonesty Performative belief Authority dependence Narrative obedience A society that treats disconfirmation as progress and implication as responsibility produces: Learning Accountability Adaptation Moral agency Genuine sovereignty The difference is not intelligence.
