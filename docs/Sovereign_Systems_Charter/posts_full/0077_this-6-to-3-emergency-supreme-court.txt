This 6 to 3 Emergency Supreme Court Ruling is BIG harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in This 6 to 3 Emergency Supreme Court Ruling is BIG Helping Us Get Back To Our Roots By Putting Limits On The Regional Federal Judiciary To Effect Nationwide Injunctions (and more) harry blazer Jan 25, 2026 2 Share So basically, the Supreme Court said that, in sync with constitutional intent, a judicial jurisdiction can only pass judgment on the issues in front of them and those judgments can only effect the people who are directly involved in that specific action in that specific jurisdiction as well. While this will create discontinuities for a while, this prevents folks with money and/or an agenda from being able to pick and choose a sympathetic Federal Judge in a single jurisdiction to acquire a ruling that then effects all 50 states. But further more, it forces the legislatures to do their jobs. And in areas where an issue will effect all states, then the federal legislature needs to do its job. No longer can authority that should be vested in the legislature be abdicated to or kidnapped by the judiciary. And this also means that the Supreme Court will have to figure out how to become more efficient in dealing with discrepancies between states over issues where the judiciary has legitimate constitutional oversight and that effect every state. 2 Share Previous Next Discussion about this post Comments Restacks Top Latest Discussions No posts Ready for more?
