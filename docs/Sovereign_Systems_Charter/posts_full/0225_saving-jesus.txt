Saving Jesus - by harry blazer - harry’s Substack harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Saving Jesus Reclaiming Christ Consciousness from 1,700 Years of Spiritual Empire harry blazer Nov 15, 2025 1 Share Prologue What We Lost, and Why We Must Reclaim It We — humanity — inherit a paradox so deep it has shaped our spiritual identity for nearly two millennia. We have been taught that a man named Jesus represents the highest spiritual path available to us, yet we have never been allowed to know him as a human being. The human Jesus — the aspirant, the ethical leader, the sovereign mind aligned with The Light — was buried under an imperial narrative that turned him from a courageous teacher into a supernatural king. In his place we were given: a divine monarch instead of a human exemplar a theology of guilt instead of a path of sovereignty an institution to obey instead of a conscience to honor a narrative of suffering instead of a practice of alignment a system of control instead of a model of awakening What we lost was not simply historical accuracy. We lost the image of a human being who aspired to align with The Light and challenged us to do the same. This manifesto is not an attack on spirituality. It is a rescue mission. We are saving Jesus from the empire that absorbed him — and saving ourselves in the process. Part I The Human Jesus and the Architecture of The Light Jesus was a human being, not a supernatural figure. He lived in poverty, confronted predation, wrestled with fear, sought clarity, cultivated conscience and aspired toward alignment with The Light. He was not perfect. He was not omniscient. He was not divine. What made him extraordinary was his intention : choosing compassion over indifference courage over fear justice over convenience non-predation over dominance or submission responsibility over victimhood conscience over authority His greatness emerged from aspiration, not perfection. This is what empire feared most. Part II How Jesus Was Buried Under 1,700 Years of Spiritual Empire 1. A Vacuum Invites Reinvention Jesus left no writings. No contemporary accounts. No institutional legacy. No defined doctrine. A human life became empty canvas — and empire filled it. 2. Early Diversity Was Intolerable to Power Early followers held diverse interpretations: reformer mystic ethical teacher social critic prophet visionary Empire cannot tolerate diversity. It collapses many voices into one. 3. Councils Consolidated Power Councils selected some texts, rejected others, punished dissent, defined orthodoxy and fused theology with political necessity. This was not spirituality. It was administration. 4. Divinization Erased the Human Jesus By turning Jesus into: perfect omniscient cosmic unique exclusive supernatural the empire made him impossible to emulate. A divine Jesus cannot model human awakening. 5. The Crucifix Became the Tool of Control Jesus was publicly murdered for all to see — a Roman spectacle designed to silence a nation. The Church later turned this tool of intimidation into a sacred icon, normalizing suffering and glorifying submission. 6. The Doctrine of Sin Manufactured Dependence Humans were declared inherently defective — a claim Jesus never made. Broken people require intermediaries. Sovereign people do not. 7. What We Lost We lost: Jesus the human aspirant Jesus the non-predatory teacher Jesus the sovereign conscience Jesus the ethical innovator Jesus the resistor of domination We received a system instead. Part III Christ Consciousness: A Universal Human Possibility, Not a Religious Monopoly Christ consciousness is not divinity. It is not perfection. It is not metaphysical identity. It is the human aspiration to align with The Light through: compassion courage conscience responsibility non-predation justice sovereignty over victimhood This state is found in every tradition. Jesus did not bring it. He pointed to it. He sought to exemplify it. Part IV The Inner Church vs the Imperial Church The Inner Church The Inner Church is the lineage of awakened conscience — people across history who chose: compassion non-predation justice humility authenticity responsibility sovereignty It predates Christianity and flows through every tradition. The Imperial Church The Imperial Church is the system built to control awakening through: guilt exclusivity obedience hierarchy fear dependency monopoly on salvation The two have waged a 1,700-year civil war inside Christianity. Part V How Spiritual Empire Sought to Break the Human Spirit** Empire used psychological engineering: declare humanity defective make the defect incurable without the institution enforce obedience as holiness monopolize salvation weaponize exclusivity sacralize trauma suppress conscience manufacture victimhood This machinery nearly conquered the human spirit but not entirely. Part VI Reconstructing Jesus: The Human Path of Aspiration Toward The Light To reclaim Jesus, we must restore him to: his humanity his essence his struggle his discernment his courage his refusal to submit his alignment with The Light through aspiration his non-predatory ethics his rejection of the victim mentality that allowed victimization He was murdered because he lived as a sovereign human being. A sovereign human being aligned with The Light can be murdered, not the human spirit. Part VII The Light Before Jesus: Why Christ Consciousness Is Older Than Christianity The Light is not Christian. It is not religious. It is not owned. It is the ethical and spiritual orientation that has guided the best among us since the beginning: The best humans among us have always: strove for alignment with The Light aspired toward goodness cultivated conscience resisted predation celebrated ethical role models attempted to create a rising tide that uplifted body, mind, heart and spirit Christ consciousness is part of humanity’s deep inheritance not a Christian innovation. When we recognize that the Light is older, broader and deeper than Christianity, the imperial edifice collapses. Part VIII The Public Murder of Jesus How Empire Uses Spectacle to Instill the Victim Mentality Empire does not simply kill rebels. It performs their deaths. The public murder of Jesus was psychological warfare meant to silence a nation instill fear validate obedience destroy aspiration and teach the victim mentality. The Church later sacralized this trauma and made it central to worship, turning politics into theology. The meaning of the spectacle is not fixed. We reclaim it. A human aligned with The Light can be murdered, not the human spirit. Part IX From Spiritual Empire to Spiritual Sovereignty Sovereignty Is Our Mandate, Our Legacy, Our Responsibility Sovereignty is not arrogance. It is the natural stance of an awakened human being. Conscience Is the Final Authority Nothing outranks conscience. Jesus never subordinated conscience to power. Non-Predation Is the Highest Ethical Standard We do not exploit dominate initiate harm or tolerate predation yet we defend ourselves proportionately when threatened. Victimhood Is the Cage Empire needed us to be victims. Jesus fought to free people from spiritual domination and from the victim mentality that allowed for victimization. Alignment Is a Practice Alignment is not belief. Not doctrine. Not membership. It is daily ethical intention. Scripture Is Human Attempt, Not Divine Dictation Scriptures are not the word of God. They are human attempts to understand the divine hope for human becoming to articulate what it means to live righteously and to align with The Light. They capture aspiration, not command. The Light Is Within We reach inward, and thus upward. The Inner Church Is Rising Humanity is remembering itself. Part X Saving Jesus: A Manifesto for the Sovereignty of Humanity** We declare: We are sovereign beings capable of aligning with The Light without intermediaries without fear without guilt without submission. We reclaim Jesus as a human who aspired toward the Light and reclaim ourselves as humans capable of the same aspiration. We reject spiritual empire and embrace the ethics of non-predation, courage and responsibility. We honor scripture as human striving not divine command. We affirm that the Light is older than all religions and belongs to humanity. We step into the lineage of the Inner Church the river of awakened humans who choose conscience over authority compassion over cruelty sovereignty over victimhood responsibility over obedience. We proclaim that the human spirit cannot be murdered. We declare that the age of spiritual dependency is over and the age of spiritual sovereignty has begun. This is the manifesto. This is the reclamation. This is the liberation. This is Saving Jesus .
