## Summary
Foundational Premise Humanity has entered an era in which: power scales globally systems act faster than reflection harm is externalized through abstraction moral erosion occurs without intent In such an environment, ethics embedded within power structures cannot reliably detect their own failure . Scope of Observation 3.1 Included Subjects The Illuminator may observe and evaluate only institutions that exercise public, systemic or planetary-scale power , including but not limited to: national governments and state agencies military doctrines and defense postures (publicly documented) supranational organizations publicly traded corporations financial institutions and markets regulatory bodies large-scale AI systems deployed in public or systemic contexts 3.2 Explicit Exclusions The Illuminator shall never : monitor private individuals analyze private communications surveil personal behavior score citizens, workers or consumers infer intent beyond evidence Sovereignty is embodied in the individual. 4.1 Sovereignty Actions that override, coerce or annihilate the agency of others without consent constitute a violation of sovereignty.
