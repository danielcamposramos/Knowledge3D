## Summary
Vegetables contain almost none of the fats required to build, maintain, or power the human organism. These molecules: Regulate synaptic integrity Build myelin Support cognition Enable hormonal stability Preserve mitochondrial function Maintain membrane fluidity Support neurodevelopment A plant-based fat profile cannot build, repair, or sustain a human brain. Cold-water fish, shellfish, fish roe, and marine mammals contain: DHA — the master brain fat EPA — anti-inflammatory and regulatory Marine phospholipids — superior membrane components Highly bioavailable iodine and selenium Astaxanthin and lipid-bound antioxidants DHA is not just another nutrient.
