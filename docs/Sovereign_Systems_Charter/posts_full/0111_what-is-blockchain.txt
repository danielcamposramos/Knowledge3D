And what it is not harry blazer Jan 10, 2026 2 2 Share What blockchain actually is At its core, blockchain is: a distributed append-only ledger with consensus rules for agreeing on order plus cryptographic verification of entries That’s it. It does not : think judge know truth enforce ethics guarantee correctness of inputs bind accountability to humans prevent fraud at the interface with reality It preserves records , not meaning . The single biggest category error “Blockchain guarantees truth.” No. Blockchain only guarantees that once a claim is recorded, it is difficult to alter without detection . If the input is false, corrupt, coerced or fabricated: blockchain faithfully preserves the lie forever with cryptographic integrity This is not a flaw — it’s a design feature . Garbage in → immutably preserved garbage. Where the bullshit enters The misinformation usually takes one of these forms: A. Confusing immutability with truth Immutability means: the record is hard to change It does not mean: the record corresponds to reality the event happened as claimed the signer was acting freely the system was not gamed upstream This confusion is deliberate in many pitches. B. Smuggling in authority under the guise of decentralization Blockchain is often sold as: “Trustless” In practice: you still trust oracles developers governance structures miners / validators protocol maintainers upgrade authorities Trust is not eliminated. It is relocated and obscured . Which is worse. C. Assigning it ethical or sovereign properties Claims like: “Blockchain enforces fairness” “Blockchain enables self-sovereign identity” “Blockchain decentralizes power” “Blockchain prevents abuse” These are non-sequitur claims . A ledger cannot: assess coercion detect deception understand context assign moral responsibility ensure consent Those are human faculties , not computational ones. The identity delusion “Self-sovereign identity on blockchain” is one of the most dangerous myths. Why? Because: identity is relational , contextual, embodied, temporal blockchain identity is static, tokenized, enumerable It reduces a human to: keys credentials attestations permissions That is not sovereignty. That is node-ification . Once identity is: enumerable queryable revocable composable It becomes governable infrastructure , not personal being. What’s An Oracle Any blockchain system that interacts with reality must rely on: sensors reporters authorities attestations feeds These are called oracles . Every oracle is: corruptible coercible incentivizable attackable This is not fixable. You cannot cryptographically prove: that wheat existed that a vote was uncoerced that consent was informed that a shipment was uncontaminated that a person acted freely Blockchain cannot cross the epistemic boundary between symbol and reality. A wall is there. Why the hype persists Because blockchain is: legible to capital legible to regulators legible to automation legible to AI legible to surveillance legible to control systems And illegible to lived human nuance . So it is framed as liberation while functioning as infrastructure for abstraction and enforcement . It is: coherent, but not complete precise, but not wise immutable, but not true The uncomfortable conclusion Blockchain is excellent for: narrow accounting settlement under agreed rules timestamped records constrained environments It is catastrophic when presented as: a substitute for judgment a replacement for trust a guarantor of truth a foundation for human sovereignty Used honestly, it’s a tool. Used ideologically, it’s a Trojan horse .
