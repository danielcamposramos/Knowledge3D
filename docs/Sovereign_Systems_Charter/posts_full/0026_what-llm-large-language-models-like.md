Why AI Excels at Structure but Struggles with Debugging LLMs are strongest in domains where: principles are stable causal relationships are structural reasoning can be done from first principles knowledge changes slowly This includes: philosophy ethics systems theory governance economics legal reasoning conceptual synthesis In these domains, AI shines because it is doing what it was designed to do: mapping structure and coherence across vast conceptual spaces . - What People Mean When They Say AI “Hallucinates” When technicians say AI “hallucinates,” they are usually describing something more precise: AI can produce internally coherent explanations that are not externally verified.
- The Correct Way to Use AI (And the Wrong Way) AI should be treated as: a hypothesis generator a conceptual synthesizer a reasoning amplifier a checklist builder a second opinion AI should not be treated as: a final authority on volatile systems a replacement for hands-on expertise a substitute for current forum knowledge an oracle for undocumented bugs The strongest workflow today is hybrid: humans gather current, empirical data AI helps interpret, generalize and reason about it Not the other way around.
