It is speed. Fraud works by collapsing reflection into urgency. Attribution theft works by collapsing authorship into momentum. Automation harms by collapsing judgment into execution. In each case, the damage occurs at the seam — where a system appears coherent enough to trust, yet incomplete enough to exploit. Most failures are not caused by evil intent. They are caused by systems that outrun their moral filters. Ethics does not fail because people reject it. It fails because no pause was built in. Attribution is a pause. Consent is a pause. Human review is a pause. Speed produces output. Judgment produces meaning. Confusing the two is how we lose both. © Harry Blazer. All rights reserved.
