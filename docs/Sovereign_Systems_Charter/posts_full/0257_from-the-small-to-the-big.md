## Summary
The move from classical (equilibrium) thermodynamics to non-equilibrium thermodynamics was itself a kind of intellectual “phase transition.” It expanded our view from static systems tending toward rest to dynamic systems sustained by flow — the domain in which life, weather, and evolution actually occur. The leap to non-equilibrium thermodynamics Beginning in the mid-20th century, Ilya Prigogine and others extended thermodynamics to systems with energy and matter fluxes — open systems maintained far from equilibrium . Every living thing is a choreography of controlled heat flow: the flicker of electrons, the hum of molecules, the pulse of a cell maintaining its improbable order against the stillness that surrounds it.
