## Summary
Sovereignty Sovereignty The inherent condition of a human being whereby they retain agency over their body, movement, decisions, and associations, and are not subject to domination, compulsion, or control by unaccountable force. Sovereignty is: inherent, not granted indivisible at the individual level prior to law the moral foundation of all legitimate authority A system that negates a person’s ability to refuse, resist, or exit destroys sovereignty. Individual Sovereign Being Individual Sovereign Being A human person recognized as possessing inherent sovereignty, moral agency, and the right to self-defense.
