THE CASE FOR DAIRY - by harry blazer - harry’s Substack harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in THE CASE FOR DAIRY Biology, History, Culture and Sovereignty harry blazer Dec 06, 2025 1 4 Share There is a fashionable modern belief that adult humans have no business consuming milk, that dairy is “unnatural,” “non-evolutionary” or somehow a biological mistake. The truth is simpler and far more interesting: real dairy has nourished human beings for thousands of years, shaped entire civilizations and remains one of the most nutrient-dense foods available to adults today. Groups that could digest dairy gained: stable calories complete protein saturated fat bioavailable minerals essential vitamins immune factors clean hydration In environments where crops failed or hunting fluctuated, dairy meant survival, fertility, cognitive development and population stability .
