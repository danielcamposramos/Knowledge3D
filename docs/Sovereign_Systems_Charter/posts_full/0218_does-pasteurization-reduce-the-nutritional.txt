Does Pasteurization Reduce The Nutritional Value Of Dairy? harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Does Pasteurization Reduce The Nutritional Value Of Dairy? Yes - As Do All Adulterations harry blazer Nov 18, 2025 Share Pasteurization (especially UHT - ultra high heat) does ALL of the following: A. Denatures heat-sensitive proteins whey proteins immunoglobulins lactoferrin certain enzymes (alkaline phosphatase, lactoperoxidase) These proteins play roles in: mineral absorption immune signaling anti-microbial defense gut protection in children They are purposefully destroyed because their presence is used as a marker that pasteurization was effective. B. Reduces some vitamins Heat reduces: Vitamin C (small amount but still relevant) Vitamin B6 Folate Some B-complex cofactors Certain bioactive peptides Enzymatic activity that aids digestion C. Reduces bioavailability of minerals Not the minerals themselves — but the enzymes that help metabolize them. Raw milk contains alkaline phosphatase , which is crucial for calcium absorption . Pasteurization destroys it. D. Alters fats High heat oxidizes certain delicate fatty acids. UHT (“ultra-pasteurized”) heat-treats at 280°F and causes: increased lipid oxidation formation of advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) changes to milk’s structural integrity These have downstream inflammatory implications. E. Eliminates beneficial bacteria Raw milk from clean, grass-fed herds contains: Lactobacillus Bifidobacterium Other probiotic species Pasteurization wipes them out. This is not trivial — it profoundly alters gut interaction. 2) Is raw dairy healthier than pasteurized, IF produced cleanly? For many people, yes — in several ways. A. Easier to digest Raw milk contains: active lactase phosphatase lipase proteases intact immunoglobulins These help break down lactose, fats, and proteins. This is why many people who “can’t tolerate milk” tolerate raw milk perfectly. B. More immune factors Raw dairy contains active: IgG IgA lactoferrin lysozyme bioactive peptides These are inactivated by heat. C. Better mineral absorption Raw milk = calcium + phosphatase + magnesium + vitamin K2 (if grass-fed) Pasteurized milk = calcium without enzymatic helpers D. Better for gut health Raw dairy is a true fermentable food substrate containing diverse bacteria. Pasteurized dairy is sterile. E. Weston A. Price observed stronger bones and teeth in raw-dairy-using cultures Swiss and Gaelic communities consuming: raw milk raw butter raw cheese raw cream …had spectacular dental and skeletal development. That isn’t accidental. 3) Has the risk of raw dairy been overstated? A. Raw dairy like any food that is mistreated CAN carry pathogens Especially from: unsanitary conditions industrial herd management animals fed unnatural diets poor milking hygiene These pathogens can include: Salmonella E. coli O157 Listeria Campylobacter So raw milk’s safety depends entirely on quality from farm to fork. B. But the danger has been overstated in policy and media Because: historically, raw milk from sick cows in filthy urban dairies did cause disease modern raw dairies that follow grass-fed, low-density, clean-handling practices have extremely low incidence of illness pasteurization became a regulatory default for industrial dairies lobbyists for big milk processors fought strongly against raw milk laws raw milk outbreaks are statistically rare compared to outbreaks from: produce (lettuce, spinach) poultry eggs peanut butter cantaloupe The CDC’s framing often lumps industrial raw milk outbreaks from a century ago with modern artisanal production , which is misleading. C. Anthropological fact: humans consumed raw dairy for 9,000 years And still do across: Alps Caucasus East Africa Mongolia Middle East These populations display strong bone structure and remarkable dental form , consistent with Price’s findings. 4) “Heat destroys nutrients” — Is that broadly true? Yes. Absolutely. With nuance. Heat-sensitive nutrients include: Vitamin C Some B vitamins Certain polyphenols Enzymes Immunoglobulins Fatty acids (oxidation) Probiotics Delicate proteins Hormones Growth factors It does not destroy minerals, but often lowers bioavailability . General rule: The more heat and processing, the lower the nutrient integrity. This is true for: milk eggs meat vegetables fruit oils grains fermented foods And it is especially true for: UHT milk canned foods ultra-processed products seed oils anything sterilized at extreme heat 5) A Fundamental Nutritional Principle Whenever safely possible, foods eaten closer to their raw, whole or minimally cooked state retain more nutrients, more enzymes, more structure and more vitality. But with two caveats: Some foods given our current supply chains are safer when cooked (e.g., pork, poultry) Some nutrients become more bioavailable with moderate cooking (e.g., lycopene, collagen → gelatin) So: Raw Dairy Safely Produced Raw or lightly cooked meats Lightly cooked vegetables Minimally processed fats = The closest diet to ancestral biological design And Aligns Perfectly With: Weston A. Price’s field data Evolutionary biochemistry What we covered about meat, organs, fat-soluble vitamins Dental and skeletal epigenetics Indigenous maternal diet strategies BUT, everything we have covered about ancestral nutrition, Weston Price, organ meats, raw/mild processing, and traditional food ways has an unspoken prerequisite: FOOD QUALITY IS A PRIMARY DETERMINANT OF FOOD SAFETY The food itself must be biologically, ecologically and chemically true . Thus, any claim about nutrition, digestion, ancestral diets, or the value of raw/organ foods must be qualified by this principle: Food must come from animals and plants raised, fed and processed in alignment with nature — or the entire analysis breaks. SPECIFICALLY: For any nutritional analysis to be valid, the food must be natural, non-GMO, free of prophylactic antibiotics, free of artificial vaccination residues, free of synthetic chemicals, raised regeneratively and processed cleanly without heat or additives. Almost all modern dietary problems and food-safety issues arise precisely because these principles are ignored THE PREREQUISITES IN DETAIL: 1. Non-GMO — both the animals and what they eat A cow fed GMO corn, soy, cottonseed meal, or industrial byproducts is: physiologically different nutritionally different biochemically stressed immunologically burdened The tissues it produces (milk, fat, muscle, organs) reflect that biology. Weston Price found that animals eating poor feed passed poor nutrition through their milk, meat, and organs . Nothing substitutes for species-appropriate diets. 2. Absolutely no prophylactic antibiotics Their use creates: altered gut flora in the animal drug residues in tissues antibiotic-resistant bacteria suppression of immune development lower nutrient density distorted amino acid profiles Pastured animals without prophylactic antibiotics develop: stronger microbiomes better mineral profiles richer fat-soluble vitamin content healthier tissues This is ancestral reality. 3. No vaccines that alter the biochemical profile of the animal Especially those involving: novel adjuvants mRNA or RNA-like platforms nanoparticle carriers immune-modulating residues Even if the final tissue carries “low or undetectable biological activity,” the systemic load on the animal affects: metabolism immune stress oxidative load inflammatory tone nutrient allocation fat composition Humans evolved eating animals that lived in complex ecological harmony , not animals under chronic immune manipulation. 4. No synthetic pesticides or herbicides (glyphosate especially) Glyphosate disrupts: shikimate pathways (in bacteria—our microbiome depends on them) mineral chelation mitochondrial function liver detoxification sulfur metabolism collagen synthesis Plants grown in glyphosate-infused soils are biochemically crippled , even if outwardly “fresh.” Animals eating glyphosate-contaminated feed pass that load into humans. This breaks the entire logic chain of “nutrient-dense diets.” 5. No irradiation, no chemical sterilization Irradiation: destroys vitamins fractures delicate proteins kills probiotic organisms oxidizes fats causes secondary breakdown products not found in nature It creates “sterile calories,” not food. 6. No additives whatsoever Anything added is: an industrial chemical a behavioral manipulator an anti-nutrient a preservative an emulsifier an artificial stabilizer Many emulsifiers and stabilizers directly: damage gut mucosa alter the microbiome cause intestinal permeability If it wasn’t part of the ancestral human diet, it is a stressor by definition. 7. Regenerative agriculture is not optional — it is the real baseline Traditional peoples: built soil enriched ecosystems increased biodiversity rotated grazing maintained perennial systems treated soil as a living organism True nutritional density comes from: mineral-rich soil microbial diversity pasture growth cycles nutrient cycling absence of chemical sterilization Every nutrient we’ve discussed — A, D, K2, DHA, minerals, antioxidants — depends on soil and ecological health. You cannot separate diet from ecology. 8. Safe processing environments matter Many (perhaps most) food safety issues arise precisely because food is produced in artificial, concentrated, chemically dependent, pathogen-friendly systems. Raw foods are not the problem. Industrial food and processes are the problem. Traditional food preparation: was clean decentralized small-scale ecology-aligned temperature-aware handled by people in community consumed quickly Industrial food processing: concentrates pathogens mixes materials from thousands of animals stores food unnaturally long uses chemical disinfectants relies on antibiotics invites contamination This is why regulators target raw foods: they assume the only environment is the industrial one — the very environment humans did not evolve in. If the food is industrially distorted anywhere in its journey from farm to fork, the biology breaks.
