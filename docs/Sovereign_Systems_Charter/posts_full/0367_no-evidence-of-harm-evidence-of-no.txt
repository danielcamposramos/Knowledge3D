How Funding, Liability and Power Shape &quot;Evidence&quot; harry blazer Jul 08, 2025 Share 1. Thesis &quot;No evidence of harm&quot; ≠ &quot;Evidence of no harm.&quot; This distinction is critical. Many controversial or suppressed areas of science—such as vaccine safety, water structuring, or electromagnetic sensitivity—are not discredited by evidence. They are discredited by lack of investigation , stemming from political, legal and financial disincentives to pursue them. 2. Why &quot;No Evidence&quot; Often Means &quot;No Research&quot; Funding bias : Research dollars overwhelmingly flow toward projects that fit dominant commercial or institutional priorities (e.g., pharmaceuticals, genetic research). Gatekeeping by institutions : NIH, CDC, and academia prioritize studies that reinforce the status quo; &quot;fringe&quot; topics are systematically ignored. Historical precedents : Paradigm-shifting ideas like continental drift, neuroplasticity or the gut-brain axis were ignored or ridiculed before accumulating enough outside-the-system momentum. 3. Case Study: Suppressed Hepatitis B Vaccine Study (CDC, 1999) RFK Jr.'s claim : In a 2025 interview with Tucker Carlson, Robert F. Kennedy Jr. referred to an internal CDC study from 1999 which allegedly found a 1135% increase in autism among children who received the hepatitis B vaccine within the first 30 days of life . Suppression of results : According to Kennedy, the study was never published because its conclusions contradicted the government narrative that vaccines are &quot;safe and effective.&quot; Mainstream silence : No record of the study has been made available; no peer-reviewed analysis has been released and no rebuttal study has explained or debunked the alleged findings. Instead, the claim is dismissed as a &quot;conspiracy theory&quot; without formal investigation. 4. Legal and Regulatory Framework: Shielding from Scrutiny 1986 NCVIA (National Childhood Vaccine Injury Act) : Declared vaccines &quot;unavoidably unsafe.&quot; Removed liability from manufacturers. Created VAERS and the Vaccine Court (which has paid out billions in injury settlements). CDC conflicts of interest : The agency is both a regulator and a vaccine purchaser/promoter—a conflict of interest that discourages critical internal review. Research gaps : No long-term, double-blind, vaccinated vs. unvaccinated population studies. No systematic investigation of synergistic toxicity in multi-dose vaccine schedules. 5. Parallel Case: Water Structuring & Biodynamic Agriculture Anthroposophical practices such as vortex stirring and flowform energizing are widely used in biodynamic farming to enhance water's &quot;life force&quot; and transfer informational qualities. Ignored, not debunked : Mainstream science has not conducted rigorous studies on these practices. No studies, no evidence : The dismissal of these methods as pseudoscience is not based on failure to demonstrate efficacy—but on the complete absence of funded inquiry. 6. Broader Implications: Science as a Political Economy Regulatory capture : Institutions like the FDA and CDC are entangled with the industries they are meant to regulate. Liability insulation : Shielding corporations from legal consequences disincentivizes transparency and rigorous safety research. Monopoly on credibility : Peer review and funding access act as control mechanisms to marginalize inconvenient findings. . 7. Conclusion Mainstream science today operates within a narrow corridor of permissible inquiry. Topics that threaten institutional legitimacy, legal liability, or industrial profit margins are labeled &quot;settled science&quot; not because they have been proven safe or ineffective, but because they have not been investigated . It is time to restore integrity by funding the science that institutions won’t touch—not in defiance of science, but in defense of its true spirit. &quot;No evidence of harm&quot; ≠ &quot;Evidence of no harm.&quot; Until we research the forbidden, we cannot claim to know the truth. harry’s Substack is a reader-supported publication.
