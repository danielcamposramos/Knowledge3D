He founded Silicon Alley Reporter, co-founded Weblogs, Inc. (acquired by AOL), and later launched the LAUNCH ecosystem, including the LAUNCH Festival and This Week in Startups podcast, where he interviews founders, investors and tech leaders about innovation and startups. Jason Calacanis visited Tesla’s Optimus robotics lab in early January 2026, where he observed the Optimus Gen 3 (V3) prototype alongside CEO Elon Musk. The visit took place on a Sunday morning at 10 a.m., and Calacanis noted the lab was highly active, with a large team of engineers working intensively—highlighting the project’s strategic importance. After the visit, Calacanis made a bold prediction: “Nobody will remember that Tesla ever made a car.” He believes the Optimus robot will become Tesla’s defining legacy, calling it “the most transformative technology product ever made in the history of humanity.” He cited the robot’s advanced integration of large language models (LLMs) , enabling it to understand and interact with the real world autonomously. Calacanis stated that Musk plans to produce a billion Optimus units , potentially achieving a 1:1 ratio of robots to humans . He added that the progress he witnessed suggests Tesla has already secured a significant lead in humanoid robotics. Elon Musk responded to these remarks on X (formerly Twitter), simply stating, “Probably true.” I have been spending a lot of time and words on the issue of AI (which I have renamed “Artificial Instinct”). As it turns out this name still applies to AI driven robots and also AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) when it gets here. We will discuss this more in a bit. But the deployment of robots at scale for both civilian, industrial and military applications, changes the game. AI now becomes embodied, is no longer confined to the cloud, but now interact with and learn from humans in the physical domain. The significance of this cannot be underestimated. And as ill prepared as we are for AI invading the human domain, we are even less so for when the robots come.
