## Summary
Historically, every great surrender of human sovereignty follows the same pattern: a fractured society encounters an entity that promises order, clarity and resolution— if only authority is centralized and obedience is simplified . Authority Always Arrives as Help Humans do not surrender sovereignty to authority because they are weak. Because for the first time, authority does not merely claim to know better—it demonstrably calculates better within constrained domains.
