## Summary
We are constructing systems that treat all life as an engineering problem, in which humans and nature are viewed simultaneously as targets to be acted upon and constraints to be overcome. Even absent intent toward tyranny, the outcome will be the elimination of human and natural sovereignty unless explicit limits are placed on what may be prototyped, optimized, automated or owned. It is an AI-mediated governance gestalt : an emergent environment formed by converging incentives across government, corporate, scientific, educational and social institutions, all operating under a shared belief system about optimization, efficiency and expertise.
