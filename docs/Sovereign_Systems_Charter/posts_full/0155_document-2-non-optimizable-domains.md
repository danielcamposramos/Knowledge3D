- Criteria for non-optimizable domains A domain is non-optimizable if optimization would: Eliminate or preempt individual agency Convert intrinsic value into instrumental value Replace consent with prediction Reduce living processes to owned systems Collapse meaning into utility Transform stewardship into control If optimization requires domination, the domain is off-limits.
- Prohibited Activities (Explicit) The following activities are incompatible with sovereignty: Behavioral prediction used for governance Ownership claims over life, identity or cognition Optimization of compliance Automation of punishment or exclusion Infrastructure that eliminates meaningful opt-out Systems designed to render resistance inefficient or invisible If a system’s success depends on eliminating dissent, it is illegitimate.
