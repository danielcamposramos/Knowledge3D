What protein-folding AI actually did: Analyze massive amounts of known sequences Infer geometric constraints Predict likely folding patterns using physics and statistics This is extraordinary — but it’s still within the domain of: existing physical laws existing chemical constraints existing amino acid behavior existing biological structures It is not the creation of a new possibility space. “AI Invented a New Vaccine or Creature — Isn’t That the Non-Possible?” This seems compelling because: the vaccine sequence never existed before the synthetic organism never existed before the DNA arrangement is new the output is novel, chemically real, and can be engineered into existence So people jump to the conclusion: “AI created something that didn’t exist — therefore AI reached into the non-possible.” But this confuses novel combination with ontological innovation .
