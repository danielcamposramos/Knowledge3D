Because there is no greater threat to humanity and human sovereignty, nor ever has there been, in the history of our species – on par with historic natural cataclysms. As with the Great Flood, or the alleged meteors that wiped out the dinosaurs and changed life on the planet forever, human deployment of AI has the potential to wipe out human sovereignty itself. How’s that for irony! AI’s trajectory toward eventual deployment across every human endeavor has a momentum all its own – because it is useful, because it is economic when humans are viewed as disposable, because it is seductive and because it represents an ephemeral gold mine for the few facilitators who prevail. 1 Share Previous Next Discussion about this post Comments Restacks Top Latest Discussions No posts Ready for more?
