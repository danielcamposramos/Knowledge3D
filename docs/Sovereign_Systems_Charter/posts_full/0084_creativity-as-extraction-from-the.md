## Summary
It cannot explain why great works feel inevitable once expressed, why multiple individuals often approach the same insight independently or why certain ideas seem to arrive only when conditions are right. Each human extracts meaning differently because each human is a different instrument: different nervous systems different histories different moral orientations different tolerances for ambiguity and responsibility The same latent structure, passing through different instruments, yields different expressions. Constraint Is Not the Enemy This model also clarifies something modern culture resists: constraint is not the opposite of creativity .
