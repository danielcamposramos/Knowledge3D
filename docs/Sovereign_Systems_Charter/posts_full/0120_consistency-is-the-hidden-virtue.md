## Summary
It asks a simpler and more rigorous question: Who performed more consistently against the same opponents, under the same constraints, over time? And in questions of human sovereignty, consistency is what anchors responsibility across time, rather than allowing it to be rewritten retroactively by outcomes. But systems that honor consistency are systems that honor reality — because reality does not grade on excitement.
