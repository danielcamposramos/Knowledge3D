Shining Light on the Concept of Framing - by harry blazer harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Shining Light on the Concept of Framing In A Few Words harry blazer Feb 03, 2026 1 Share A Frame is the playing field, the rules of the game, the definition of winning, and the authority to change or end the game. Machines can play the game extremely well. Humans are responsible for defining the game, revising the rules and stopping play when the game itself no longer makes sense. Humans are the guardians of frames: the designers of missions, values and constraints. Machines execute; they are systems that perform a specified mission within the frames they are given. Without this clarification, people will say: “Fine — just make better rules.” But rules are themselves value judgments. And deciding when rules no longer apply is not a rule-following act. That’s guardianship. Machines play games. Humans must decide which games are worth playing.
