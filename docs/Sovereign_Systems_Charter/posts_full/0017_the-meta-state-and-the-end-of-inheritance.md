## Summary
Instead, it appears as a constellation of trends: mass migration unmanaged by consent, the erosion of national sovereignty, the moral delegitimization of inherited culture, the rise of supranational authority, and the accelerating push toward transhumanist “enhancement.” Individually, each trend is debated. As a result, sovereignty itself must be reframed — not as a legitimate expression of collective self-determination, but as a source of injustice, exclusion or instability. The same moral logic applies: Inheritance is unfair Limits are oppressive Outcomes must converge Authority must scale Transhumanism is not primarily about technological curiosity.
