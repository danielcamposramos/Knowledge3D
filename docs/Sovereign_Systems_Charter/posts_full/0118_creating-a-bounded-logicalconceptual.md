## Summary
Inside such a space: different moral traditions can operate disagreement can occur meaningfully technology can evolve responsibility can be assigned and owned Outside that space: responsibility dissolves sovereignty evaporates ethics becomes performative correction becomes impossible The boundary is not political or technical. Once the space is bounded, certain paths are excluded automatically: delegating moral judgment to systems diffusing responsibility into procedure treating optimization as ethics allowing irreversible decisions without a human moral owner These are not rejected because they are unpopular or dangerous. At the same time, the space remains open where it must: AI can assist, analyze, monitor and warn humans retain authority over moral decisions pluralism remains intact disagreement remains legitimate This is exclusion by coherence, not by decree.

## Key Sentences
- Inside such a space: different moral traditions can operate disagreement can occur meaningfully technology can evolve responsibility can be assigned and owned Outside that space: responsibility dissolves sovereignty evaporates ethics becomes performative correction becomes impossible The boundary is not political or technical.
- Once the space is bounded, certain paths are excluded automatically: delegating moral judgment to systems diffusing responsibility into procedure treating optimization as ethics allowing irreversible decisions without a human moral owner These are not rejected because they are unpopular or dangerous.
- At the same time, the space remains open where it must: AI can assist, analyze, monitor and warn humans retain authority over moral decisions pluralism remains intact disagreement remains legitimate This is exclusion by coherence, not by decree.
- Our framework now has three inseparable pillars : Constraint — what must hold for coherence to exist Bounded conceptual space — where ethical reasoning can occur Honest language — the medium that allows constraints to bind Remove any one of the three, and the system fails.
- By recovering constraint as a generative force — and by using it to bound our thinking rather than inflate it — we regain something essential: the ability to reason our way forward without surrendering sovereignty.
