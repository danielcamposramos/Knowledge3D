## Summary
Emergency Replaces Informed Consent Once a public health emergency is declared, the ethical center of gravity shifts: Speed outranks completeness Deployment outranks long-term data Uptake outranks individual discretion Compliance outranks deliberation This does not require claiming malicious intent. It requires only acknowledging an institutional truth: Under emergency framing, uncertainty is treated as a cost of delay rather than a signal for restraint. Just as: Software companies shipped unfinished products Chemical companies released persistent compounds COVID countermeasures were rolled out under conditions where the public bore uncertainty that would traditionally remain internal .
