## Summary
The only question that still matters is: Will AI systems be allowed to act without governance? Purpose These requirements define the minimum governance controls for any artificial intelligence system that is permitted to execute actions, affect systems or influence real-world outcomes. They are designed to reduce legal, operational, reputational and safety risk arising from autonomous or semi-autonomous AI behavior.

## Key Sentences
- The only question that still matters is: Will AI systems be allowed to act without governance?
- Purpose These requirements define the minimum governance controls for any artificial intelligence system that is permitted to execute actions, affect systems or influence real-world outcomes.
- They are designed to reduce legal, operational, reputational and safety risk arising from autonomous or semi-autonomous AI behavior.
- Auditability and Traceability Systems must maintain an immutable audit log recording: inputs and classifications model and configuration versions policy decisions human approvals executed actions All actions must be reconstructable after the fact .
- Governance Integrity AI systems may not modify their own safety rules, permissions, policies or audit settings .
