Purpose The Illuminator Pipeline translates explicit moral principles into continuous, real-time observation, analysis and alarm — without enforcement, surveillance of individuals or delegated moral authority. Its purpose is not to decide what is right, but to detect when reality diverges from declared values . 2. Design Constraints (Non-Negotiable) The pipeline must satisfy all of the following simultaneously: Non-coercive : produces information only Institution-only : no monitoring of private individuals Principle-anchored : no ad-hoc moral judgments Evidence-first : all alarms cite sources Reproducible : third parties can rerun analyses Tamper-resistant : outputs persist even under attack Legible : understandable by non-experts If any one of these fails, the system is invalid. 3. Input Layer: What Is Observed 3.1 Source Classes (Public Only) The pipeline ingests public, attributable institutional data , including: Governance legislation, regulations, executive orders budgets, appropriations, procurement contracts policy memos, white papers, strategy documents Judicial & Enforcement court filings, rulings, settlements regulatory enforcement actions immunity grants and exemptions Corporate & Financial SEC filings, earnings calls, disclosures lobbying records mergers, acquisitions, derivatives exposure bailout structures and risk transfers Military & Security (Public Domain) doctrine documents deterrence strategies escalation frameworks oversight reports AI & Technology model cards, safety reports deployment policies incident disclosures automation scopes and override mechanisms Environmental & Commons resource extraction permits pollution reports climate commitments vs outcomes No leaks. No hacked material. No private data. Illumination comes from connecting what is already visible but fragmented . 4. Principle Mapping Layer Every input is evaluated only through the lenses defined in the Constitution. 4.1 Principle-to-Signal Mapping Each moral principle maps to observable signals , for example: Sovereignty irreversible actions without appeal paths policies eliminating meaningful choice coercive dependency structures Consent opt-out impossibility consent buried in opacity “implied” participation at scale Accountability decisions without named owners automated outcomes without traceability “system did it” justifications Proportionality totalizing responses to partial threats permanent measures justified by temporary crises Reversibility automation without human override escalation paths without de-escalation point-of-no-return policies Transparency with Power secrecy correlated with consequence magnitude classification shielding wrongdoing rather than safety This mapping is explicit, versioned and public . 5. Pattern Detection & Analysis Layer This is where AI is used — carefully and bounded. 5.1 AI Functions (Permitted) AI is used to: correlate actions with stated principles detect divergence trends over time surface erosion patterns flag incentive contradictions identify escalation without restraint detect normalization of exceptions AI may: summarize cluster compare trend alert 5.2 AI Functions (Prohibited) AI shall never : infer intent assign guilt predict individual behavior optimize for outcomes decide moral truth suppress alarms AI is an instrument panel , not a pilot. 6. Alarm Generation Layer 6.1 Alarm Types Alarms are graded, not binary: Advisory – early divergence detected Warning – repeated or accelerating deviation Critical – systemic moral inversion or irreversible risk 6.2 Alarm Contents Every alarm must include: principle(s) implicated observed behavior evidence citations confidence level uncertainty notes historical context An alarm says: “This pattern is inconsistent with these principles.” It never says: “This is illegal” “This is evil” “This must stop” 7. Human Review & Stewardship Layer Before publication: alarms are reviewed by a plural human panel reviewers assess evidence sufficiency dissenting views are logged minority objections are preserved No single reviewer can suppress an alarm. Disagreement is surfaced, not hidden. 8. Publication Layer (Radical Transparency) 8.1 Outputs All findings are published as: human-readable reports machine-readable datasets versioned archives cryptographic hashes 8.2 Accessibility Reports must be: freely accessible mirrored across jurisdictions time-stamped and immutable linkable and citable Sunlight is the only enforcement. 9. Feedback, Response & Correction Subjects may: submit public responses contest evidence provide counter-data All responses: are appended, not substituted remain visible never erase the original alarm Corrections improve the system — they do not absolve history. 10. Integrity & Resilience Mechanisms To resist capture and shutdown: distributed hosting decentralized publication cryptographic verification open-source analysis code independent mirrors no central kill switch Attacks must leave evidence. 11. Relationship to Action The Illuminator: does not call for protests does not direct policy does not recommend punishment It restores moral visibility . Action remains the responsibility of: citizens institutions leaders history 12. Operating Principle Morality cannot be automated. But moral incoherence can be made impossible to ignore.
