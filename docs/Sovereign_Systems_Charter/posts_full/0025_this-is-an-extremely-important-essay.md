## Summary
If humans abdicate leadership, judgment and custodianship to AI systems, vast domains of human knowledge will not be preserved or amplified — they will disappear . The kinds of knowledge AI does not and cannot hold There are entire categories of knowledge that are structurally invisible to AI systems. Why abdication causes knowledge loss, not preservation AI systems preserve what is: formalized repeatable legible statistically reinforced rewarded by usage They shed what is: local anomalous fragile experiential socially maintained If humans step back — if judgment, troubleshooting, leadership and stewardship are outsourced — the system does not become smarter.
