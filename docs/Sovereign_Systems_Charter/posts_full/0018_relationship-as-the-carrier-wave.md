## Summary
Relationship as the Carrier Wave of Meaning harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Relationship as the Carrier Wave of Meaning harry blazer Feb 11, 2026 Share There comes a moment — often quietly, without drama — when an otherwise competent, thinking, autonomous person realizes something unsettling: Life can function perfectly well without meaning. Modern systems — economic, technological, bureaucratic — increasingly reward: speed over presence efficiency over attentiveness abstraction over embodiment scalability over intimacy Relationships resist all four. Not because systems are insufficiently advanced, but because meaning requires: mutual vulnerability unoptimized presence moral recognition accountability between agents who can refuse These properties cannot be simulated without collapsing into performance.
