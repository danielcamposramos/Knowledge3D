## Summary
After the visit, Calacanis made a bold prediction: “Nobody will remember that Tesla ever made a car.” He believes the Optimus robot will become Tesla’s defining legacy, calling it “the most transformative technology product ever made in the history of humanity.” He cited the robot’s advanced integration of large language models (LLMs) , enabling it to understand and interact with the real world autonomously. AI now becomes embodied, is no longer confined to the cloud, but now interact with and learn from humans in the physical domain. And as ill prepared as we are for AI invading the human domain, we are even less so for when the robots come.
