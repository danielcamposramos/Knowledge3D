Subscribe 🧨 The Debt Freedom Manifesto How the American People Were Enslaved by Design—and How We Break the Chains “If the American people ever allow private banks to control the issue of their currency... the banks and corporations that will grow up around them will deprive the people of all property.” —Thomas Jefferson I. We Were Not Born to Be Debtors We are sovereign beings—of flesh, will, and creative spirit. We were not born to toil under compound interest. We were not born to rent our lives from private banking syndicates. We were not born to be tracked, rated, or redeemed through QR codes. But in 1913, Congress, under the banner of “stability,” sold the soul of the republic. They passed the Federal Reserve Act , ceding the people’s sovereign right to create money to a private banking cartel . And in the same year, through the 16th Amendment , they armed that cartel with the power to tax the people directly. These were not unrelated events. They were two sides of the same coin of control . The banks could now lend unlimited sums to the government—because repayment was guaranteed, with interest, through the sweat of the people . This was the birth of eternal debt slavery , by design. II. The System Was Engineered to Enslave Most people still believe banks lend out existing money. They don’t. When a commercial bank issues a loan, it creates brand new money —out of nothing—by typing numbers into your account. But when that loan is repaid, the money disappears . It is extinguished. The only thing that remains is the interest , which was never created in the first place. To pay that interest, more loans must be issued . And thus, the system requires perpetual debt expansion to survive. The entire economy becomes a treadmill of scarcity—one that speeds up every year. In this system, stability is impossible , and freedom is mathematically prohibited . III. 2008: When the Chains Could Have Been Broken In 2008, the banks collapsed under the weight of their own fraud and speculation—particularly in the derivatives market. The housing bubble burst. Liquidity vanished. The system seized up. And the American people—who had played by the rules—were left to drown. But there was another path. The government could have taken the same trillions of dollars it used to bail out Wall Street and paid off every residential mortgage in the country . The result? Banks still get repaid . Balance sheets are restored . Homeowners are freed from debt . The money supply remains intact . The liquidity crisis would have ended— and the people would have emerged sovereign . But that didn’t happen. Instead, the Fed and Treasury shoveled over $16 trillion into the banks—much of it in secret. The people got foreclosures, job losses, and austerity. The banks got bigger, richer, and more powerful. It wasn’t a bailout. It was a heist. IV. What We’re Seeing Now Is the Same Cycle—Accelerated Over the last decade, Congress and successive presidents have deregulated banks again , allowing them to gamble on derivatives , speculate in hedge funds, and use depositor collateral to do it. Once again, the system is cracking . Banks are overleveraged and exposed to trillions in opaque derivatives. The government is insolvent —with debt service approaching $1.5 trillion/year. Foreign buyers are walking away from U.S. bonds. Interest rates are rising to attract buyers—but that just deepens the insolvency . Both the private and public sectors are on a debt treadmill with no off-ramp. And as always, the people will be told to pay for the damage. V. This Was the Plan All Along The architects of the Federal Reserve system—those who met at Jekyll Island in 1910— knew where it would lead . They understood: That debt is the ultimate control mechanism . That easy money would seduce governments and citizens alike. That war and crisis would justify ever more borrowing. That, eventually, the collateral would be the country itself —its land, labor, and natural resources. Debt was the Trojan horse . Confiscation was the goal. This is why nations that resist this system— Russia, Iran, Libya, Iraq —are treated as enemies of civilization. Not because they threaten peace. But because they threaten the monetary monopoly . VI. The Final Stage: CBDCs and Digital Serfdom Now, the solution to the crisis they created is being unveiled: Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) —programmable, surveilled, and revocable. Social credit scoring —tying your financial access to ideological compliance. Universal Basic Income —but only if you play by the rules. This isn’t money. It’s a digital leash . You won’t own your money. You’ll be issued tokens—redeemable only if you behave. No bank account? No dissent. No vaccine? No paycheck. Too much carbon? No travel. The monetary system is becoming a behavioral control grid , run by unelected central bankers, enforced by AI, and sold to the public as “equity” and “safety.” VII. What We Must Do We must name the system for what it is: Neofeudalism , where banks own everything and rent it back to you. Technocratic totalitarianism , where permission replaces rights. Economic warfare , disguised as policy. And we must reject it. We demand: The end of debt-based money creation . The abolition of the Federal Reserve , and all central banks operating above democratic accountability. The restoration of Treasury-issued sovereign currency , interest-free and debt-free. The refusal of all CBDCs and biometric ID systems. A new monetary system built on trust, transparency, and freedom —not surveillance and servitude. VIII. Final Word We are not here to rent our lives. We are not here to borrow our freedom. We are not here to digitize our consent. We are sovereign. We are human. And we do not consent. Let this be our declaration. Let this be our resistance. Let this be the beginning of the end of their system. No more debt chains disguised as solutions. No more control passed off as safety. No more digital feudalism sold as progress.
