## Summary
Article II — Moral Orientation All systems of knowledge and power shall operate under an encompassing intention: to seek the good of the whole while honoring the sovereignty of every part. Article V — Ethical Architecture Every design—technical, institutional, or cultural—must encode humility, empathy, and moral restraint as structural parameters. We have used intelligence as a weapon—against nature, against one another, and ultimately against ourselves.
