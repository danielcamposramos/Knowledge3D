- Menstrual irregularities impair bone health This is the core of the Female Athlete Triad / RED-S problem: Many young female athletes train heavily Many do not consume enough calories The body responds by suppressing ovulation That means: lower estrogen higher cortisol lower bone density impaired recovery And low estrogen at puberty is devastating because this is the window when lifelong bone density is established.
- THE SHORT TRUTH Puberty is vulnerable because during these few years: bones lengthen before they strengthen muscles lag behind bones ligaments loosen while loads increase the pelvis widens, altering mechanics neuromuscular control resets nutrition often fails to match expenditure And estrogen — the hormone that protects bone and stabilizes tissue — can fluctuate wildly or even drop to near-zero if energy intake is insufficient.
