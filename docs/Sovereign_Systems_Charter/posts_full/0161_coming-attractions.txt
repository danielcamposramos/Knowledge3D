COMING ATTRACTIONS - by harry blazer - harry’s Substack harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in COMING ATTRACTIONS WOWS FORTHCOMING harry blazer Dec 26, 2025 3 1 Share I have been spending weeks now synthesizing ideas that have been percolating within for decades. With the help of my CHAT partner, we have created a series of essays of great import that I hope will amaze you as much as they have me. These essays explore reality through the lens of information, causality and coherence. They argue that causality is a fundamental property of reality, that the speed of light constrains the propagation of meaning rather than motion, that digital systems necessarily compress what is possible and that human beings play a unique role in expanding the possible through choice, responsibility and becoming. This work is not a critique of technology, but an attempt to clarify its limits — and, in doing so, to clarify what must remain human, in order for the universe to become more meaningful.
