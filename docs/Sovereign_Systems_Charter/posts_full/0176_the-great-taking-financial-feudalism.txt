CBDCs reveal the next phase of the project: Control not just what you “own,” but what you can do. Not just your assets, but your autonomy. Not just property, but behavior. CBDCs are not about convenience. They are not about inclusion. They are not about efficiency. They are about closing the last escape hatch . They are the endgame of a century-long architecture whose purpose has been consistent and unmistakable: Centralize money Centralize credit Centralize custody Centralize crisis authority Centralize behavioral control And CBDCs sit at step 5 — the moment the system moves from harvesting wealth to harvesting sovereignty. Programmable Money Is Not Money. It Is Permission. A CBDC allows: Geographic restrictions Time restrictions Purchase restrictions Negative rates Automatic taxation Automatic fines Spending quotas Social/behavioral scoring Access suspension In other words: CBDCs turn currency into a remote-control device for human conduct. The architecture of the Great Taking was about possession . The architecture of CBDCs is about obedience . From Entitlement to Enclosure: The Return of Financial Feudalism Look at the trajectory: Phase 1 — Dematerialization Paper certificates → digital claims (You stop owning assets directly.) Phase 2 — Centralized Custody Broker → clearinghouse → Cede & Co (Ownership collapses upward.) Phase 3 — Bail-In Architecture Deposit → liability → loss-absorbing capital (Your wealth becomes systemic fuel.) Phase 4 — CBDCs Money → programmable control layer (Your behavior becomes a managed variable.) This is not the free market. This is not capitalism. This is not democracy. This is financial feudalism : A sovereign creditor class at the top A managerial priesthood enforcing compliance Serfs living inside permission-based digital enclosures The design is unmistakable. CBDCs + Great Taking = Total Spectrum Scarcity Two systems merge: The Great Taking — confiscation of assets through legal structure CBDCs — control of every future transaction through digital structure Together, they form the ultimate scarcity engine : Scarcity of property (you do not own) Scarcity of privacy (you cannot hide) Scarcity of independence (you cannot exit) Scarcity of dissent (you can be switched off) Scarcity becomes a weapon and a permanent state. Exactly as we’ve discussed before: digital systems create engineered scarcity to manage human beings as economic livestock — Nodes inside a programmable matrix. The System Is Coherent, But Not Complete What is coherent: The banking structure The creditor hierarchy The crisis architecture The custody regime The digital identity layer The CBDC framework What is not complete: The elimination of analog sovereignty The destruction of parallel economies The extinction of local networks The colonization of all non-digitized humanity The replacement of soul-based agency with algorithmic obedience The Great Taking is the seizure of property. CBDCs are the seizure of possibility. But both fail at the same boundary: The analog, the local, the human, the sovereign — the parts of life that cannot be coded, collateralized or confiscated. That is the frontier. That is the real resistance. That is what they cannot take — unless we willingly surrender it.
