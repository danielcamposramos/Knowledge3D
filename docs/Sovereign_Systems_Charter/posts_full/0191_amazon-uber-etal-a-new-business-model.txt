AMAZON, UBER, et.al. - A NEW BUSINESS MODEL FOR VOLUNTARY SERVITUDE harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in AMAZON, UBER, et.al. - A NEW BUSINESS MODEL FOR VOLUNTARY SERVITUDE To Maximize Corporate Profits and Minimize Exposure harry blazer Dec 05, 2025 Share Amazon Flex, Uber, Instacart, DoorDash — all of them represent a radical shift in the structure of labor. They have achieved what earlier corporations only dreamed of: a workforce that behaves like employees, absorbs the costs of ownership, signs away legal protections, and sees itself as “independent entrepreneurs” while being managed by algorithms more controlling than any supervisor. This is the new economic miracle: the worker bears all costs and exposure and the company owns only the interface yet takes the lion’s share of the value. 1. The Illusion of Freedom The platform promises flexibility. “No boss. Work when you want. Be your own business.” But this is the clever part. The worker doesn’t realize that freedom without power is just unmanaged risk. They have the freedom to choose which version of precarity they prefer. The algorithm decides everything that matters: price pacing routes priority deactivation The “gig worker” is a Node in a matrix where the human is reduced to a transactional endpoint in a system they do not control. Digitized systems convert humans into Nodes whose function is to serve algorithmic objectives. 2. The Externalization of Corporate Cost This is the central pillar of the model. Traditional employers pay for: vehicles maintenance insurance fuel downtime liability worker’s comp The platforms pay for: an app a server some branding Everything else is dumped onto the worker. The brilliance — and the cruelty — is that the workers volunteer for it. 3. The Hidden Negative Wage People think they’re making $18 to $25 an hour - in some cases more. They are not. There is a big difference between gross receipts (revenue) vs net profit. When you subtract: gas depreciation tires brakes extra insurance lost opportunity unpaid waiting time unpaid return-to-zone time Many workers earn below minimum wage . Some effectively earn nothing at all once wear-and-tear is properly accounted for. This is why gig work tends to attract: people who are financially stressed people who don’t calculate depreciation people who don’t understand amortization people who think cash flow equals income That’s who the system was designed for. 4. Worker as Disposable Infrastructure The worker is no longer a person — they become the capex . The company has achieved the corporate dream: no trucks no warehouses no drivers no liability no union no HR no healthcare no pensions The worker becomes the fleet. The worker becomes the warehouse. The worker becomes the shock absorber for fluctuating demand. It is a perfect business model as long as the workforce remains desperate and misinformed. 5. Algorithmic Serfdom The platforms have resurrected feudalism, but with a digital gloss. You serve the master’s land, but the land is now an app. You supply the horse, the armor and the grain. You obey the algorithmic lord who determines your worth in real time. And once again, the workers don’t leave, because the illusion of independence disguises the bondage. 6. Voluntary Servitude as a Social Technology The deepest point: No one is forcing these people. They line up for the chance to: wear out their cars chase $4 deliveries work without health and disability insurance be deactivated without appeal This is the genius of the system. The worker believes they are choosing freedom, but they are choosing someone else’s economic plan for their life. Victimization = being subsumed by a plan that is not yours. Sovereignty = retaining the ability to have your own plan. Gig work strips the worker of sovereignty while convincing them they have more of it than ever. This is why the model works. And this is why it is spreading. It is immensely profitable for the corporation. A Warning This model is not confined to delivery driving. It is the blueprint for: healthcare education consulting retail logistics and eventually, governance The sovereign human is replaced by the compliant Node. Unless we see the spell for what it is, voluntary servitude will become the default condition of the digital economy. Subscribe Please consider becoming a paid subscriber to support my efforts.
