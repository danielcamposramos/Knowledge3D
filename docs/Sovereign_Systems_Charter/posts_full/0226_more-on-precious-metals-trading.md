## Summary
Three independent physical markets are tightening simultaneously: Retail coin markets (Maple Leafs, Eagles, Krugerrands, etc.) Retail/wholesale bar markets (1 oz, 10 oz, 1 kg, and even 100 oz products) Exchange-deliverable bar markets (COMEX 100 oz bars, LBMA 400 oz bars) This is crucial to understand: retail shortages do not automatically signal wholesale shortages — but when both begin tightening at the same time, the futures markets come under pressure. Why This Matters Now We are in a moment where: gold is being remonetized by emerging nations silver is being devoured by industry physical demand is rising at every level paper markets are thin relative to global claims trust in institutions is eroding and the divergence between futures and physical is widening Whether this resolves smoothly or breaks abruptly will depend on how long exchanges can maintain confidence in paper claims. The Physical Market Is Actually Three Separate Markets The physical world is divided into: Retail coins (Maple Leafs, Eagles, Krugerrands) Retail & wholesale bars (1 oz to 1 kg) Exchange-deliverable bars (100 oz / 400 oz) These markets have different inventories, buyers, bottlenecks, and rules.
