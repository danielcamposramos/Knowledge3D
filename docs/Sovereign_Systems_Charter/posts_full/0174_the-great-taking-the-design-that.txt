This is the new form of invisible governance: Power is no longer exercised by direct action. It is exercised by the design of structures that guarantee outcomes. And because they think in decades , not election cycles — their structures outlive every attempt to regulate them. They are not omnipotent. They are simply unopposed at the structural level. This is what Prouty saw in the shadow intelligence world — but here, the scope is civilizational .
