Title: Why These Agencies Must Be Abolished: The Case for Ending the Federal Security State By Harry Blazer INTRODUCTION The American public has been conditioned to believe that federal law enforcement and intelligence agencies are the last line of defense against chaos, terror, and internal collapse. But what if the opposite is true? What if these institutions, shrouded in secrecy and insulated from public accountability, are in fact the architects of the very crises they claim to protect us from? Time and again, we see a disturbing pattern: federal agencies, particularly the FBI, ATF, and CIA, are deeply entangled in the very plots they later use to justify expansions of their power. Oklahoma City. 9/11. The 1993 WTC bombing. The Boston Marathon. January 6th. Each of these events shows signs of foreknowledge, infiltration, evidence suppression, and narrative engineering. This isn't speculation — it's structurally observable, historically recurring, and deeply corrosive to the fabric of a sovereign republic. PART I: THE MANAGED EVENT DOCTRINE These agencies no longer operate as neutral defenders of justice. They have become narrative shepherds — managing public perception, controlling political fallout, and engineering fear as a tool of governance. Their methods include: Infiltrating fringe groups, sometimes provoking or accelerating plots Suppressing surveillance footage, testimony, and inconvenient facts Rapidly promoting a lone-wolf narrative to shield institutional failures Using the aftermath to justify laws, budgets, and new surveillance powers This cycle — Problem > Reaction > Solution — is now standard operating procedure. PART II: TIMELINE OF FEDERAL COMPLICITY 1993 WTC Bombing: FBI informant Emad Salem warned of the plot and offered to use fake explosives. The FBI declined. 1995 OKC Bombing: ATF offices mysteriously empty. Eyewitnesses report a second suspect (John Doe #2), suppressed by investigators. 2001 9/11 Attacks: Hijackers tracked. Saudi links ignored. NORAD disabled via simultaneous war games. 2013 Boston Bombing: FBI interviewed suspect Tamerlan Tsarnaev after a Russian warning. No action taken. 2021 Jan 6th: Known provocateurs (e.g., Ray Epps) not prosecuted. Capitol Police stood down at key moments. FBI involvement acknowledged in congressional testimony. In each case, federal agencies had proximity, prior knowledge, or operational connections — and used the events to tighten control over citizens, information, and money. PART III: WHO'S REALLY INVOLVED? Recurring institutional players include: FBI: Entrapment, provocateurs, suppression of evidence CIA: Foreign regime change bleeding into domestic manipulation ATF: Operational blunders or staging (Waco, Ruby Ridge, OKC) DHS/Fusion Centers: Surveillance nodes merging federal with local power Private contractors: Palantir, Booz Allen, In-Q-Tel-backed entities with deep access to data and behavioral modeling These actors operate in coordination, often sharing assets and information behind closed doors. They also coordinate with corporate media and think tanks to shape the post-crisis narrative. PART IV: WHY ABOLITION, NOT REFORM, IS THE ONLY SOLUTION You cannot reform a machine whose survival depends on lying to the public, entrapping citizens, and exploiting tragedies for structural power gains. We must: Dissolve the CIA's operational wing — it should collect and coordinate intelligence, not execute covert actions. Abolish the FBI — return investigative and enforcement powers to the states and constitutional sheriffs. Eliminate the ATF and DHS — they are products of engineered crises. Ban private intelligence contractors from managing public infrastructure Protect whistleblowers as sacred actors in democratic preservation This is not vengeance. It is preservation. Of truth. Of sovereignty. Of safety. CONCLUSION: THE CASE FOR CIVIL REFOUNDING Every empire cultivates its own security apparatus. And every apparatus, left unchecked, becomes a parasitic force that feeds on its own people. The federal security state is not a malfunction. It is the intended result of decades of slow centralization, narrative control, and capital flow manipulation — both overt and covert. It cannot be fixed from within. It must be dismantled, disarmed, and replaced with transparent, decentralized, and accountable systems rooted in local sovereignty and natural law.
