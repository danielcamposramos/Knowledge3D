## Summary
Instead, we are offered process innovations, accounting maneuvers and rhetorical reassurances — all designed to keep liquidity flowing without confronting the moral and structural failure at the core of the system. The system now requires debt expansion to maintain baseline liquidity in the real economy, while simultaneously providing non-debt liquidity and subsidies to the financial sector through central banking facilities, regulatory privileges and emergency backstops. The Quiet Choice: Debasement Over Restraint At every critical juncture, the US government has made the same choice: Not spending restraint Not transparent reprioritization Not coherent accounting But currency degradation .
