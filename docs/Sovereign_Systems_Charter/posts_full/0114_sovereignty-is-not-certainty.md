- The moral logic is familiar: “The guidelines required it.” “The experts decided.” “The model says this is optimal.” In this framework, no one is fully responsible — and therefore no one is accountable.
- But without discipline, it mutates into something else: “I see what others cannot.” “Consensus is always wrong.” “My intuition is proof.” “My certainty replaces their certainty.” At this point, authority has not been rejected — it has merely been internalized .
- The Hard Position True sovereignty occupies an uncomfortable middle ground.
- It does not say: “The institution knows.” “I know.” It says: “I choose.” “I may be wrong.” “I remain responsible regardless.” This position is demanding.
