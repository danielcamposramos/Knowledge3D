Deeper Look Into Subsidized Legal Immunity harry’s Substack Subscribe Sign in Deeper Look Into Subsidized Legal Immunity Liability Immunity Did Not Start With Pharma harry blazer Jan 04, 2026 1 Share The widespread belief that pharmaceuticals uniquely enjoy liability shields is historically false. The nuclear industry received the earliest, strongest and most explicit federal immunity regime in US industrial history. A Quick Review of The Template The Price-Anderson Act (1957): Caps liability for nuclear operators Socializes catastrophic risk Makes the federal government the insurer of last resort Applies to both military and civilian nuclear operations This regime was demanded by contractors like DuPont , because: Risks were unquantifiable Damages were potentially infinite No private insurance market would participate Without Price–Anderson, nuclear power does not exist. That is an uncontested fact. Why this matters Price–Anderson created the legal doctrine that: If an industry is strategically important enough, the public must absorb its worst failures. This doctrine later migrates. PFAS: Nuclear Logic Applied to Chemistry Persistent Risk, Delayed Accountability PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) are not an accident. They are the chemical expression of the same logic . What PFAS are Extremely stable synthetic compounds Resistant to heat, water, oil and degradation Bioaccumulative Effectively permanent once released They were ideal for: Military firefighting foams Aerospace Electronics Coatings Industrial processes In other words: strategic materials . DuPont’s role DuPont was among the primary developers and manufacturers of PFAS compounds (including PFOA/C8 used in Teflon) - which is why I never use coated pans). Internal documents show: Early awareness of persistence Early evidence of bioaccumulation Long delay before public disclosure The resemblance to nuclear waste is not rhetorical: BothNuclear waste and PFAS share an identical eco-profile: Long-lived, Invisible, Bio-hazardous, Requires perpetual management, Cleanup costs socialized PFAS are chemical radiation. Not metaphorically — structurally. Monsanto Is Not Separate It Is Interwoven There is deep interconnectivity between DuPont and Monsanto - arguably the two primary industrial polluters of the modern era . This is not conjecture. It is documented through partnerships, mergers, shared technologies and regulatory alignment . 1. Direct Corporate Integration In 2017, DuPont merged with Dow to form DowDuPont DowDuPont later split into: Dow (materials) DuPont (specialty products) Corteva (agriculture) Corteva absorbed DuPont’s agrochemical and seed businesses , which had long-standing overlap with Monsanto’s domain. This is not coincidence. It is industry consolidation under regulatory pressure . 2. Monsanto and DuPont: Competitive Twins, Not Opposites Historically: Monsanto focused on: Herbicides (e.g. glyphosate) GM seeds DuPont focused on: Chemical precursors Materials science Agricultural chemicals seeds (Pioneer) including GM seeds They: Shared regulatory pathways Used similar toxicological frameworks Benefited from revolving-door science Relied on regulatory capture rather than public transparency Different brands. Same chemical-industrial logic . Monsanto’s Fate Proves the Pattern Monsanto eventually became: Too visible Too litigated Too politically radioactive It was acquired by Bayer , exporting liability while preserving product lines. This mirrors DuPont’s behavior: spin, split, rebrand, distribute liability, preserve knowledge Visibility kills. Infrastructure survives. The Key Insight From nuclear reactors to PFAS to agrochemicals, the same structure repeats: High-risk chemistry Strategic or military justification Early knowledge of long-term harm Delayed disclosure Legal shielding or capped liability Public absorbs cleanup Corporation restructures and persists This is not ordinary malice. It is institutionalized moral hazard . Synthesis The nuclear industry taught American law that some risks are too important to be owned. PFAS taught industry that the same logic could be applied quietly to everyday life. Monsanto proved that when visibility becomes dangerous, liability can be exported, while chemistry endures. Why This Is Central to the War You Described We have maintained that we have been forced into a war for the sovereignty and soul of humanity. That war is not waged with bombs (at first). It is waged with: Invisible harms Deferred consequences Statistical fog Legal immunities Institutional memory that outlives accountability All for profit. DuPont is not the villain.
