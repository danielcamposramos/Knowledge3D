- The error of treating constraints as hidden laws Modern systems — especially computational ones — tend to treat constraints as: rules to be enforced boundaries to be optimized against parameters to be satisfied From inside such systems, constraint and determination look indistinguishable.
- A system that can only optimize will always mistake: constraint for control structure for destiny coherence for closure This is why AI systems are so easily framed as “inevitable.” They are not inevitable.
- Why this matters for sovereignty Sovereignty depends on three things: real alternatives meaningful exclusion responsibility for choice Constraints preserve all three.
- And this is why systems that operate deterministically (or approximate it through optimization) can never be sovereign, no matter how intelligent they become.
