# PM-KR Community Group Wiki

**Welcome to the PM-KR (Procedural Memory Knowledge Representation) Community Group collaborative workspace.**

This wiki serves as a cross-CG collaboration hub, implementing Milton Ponson's proposal for shared knowledge infrastructure across W3C Community Groups.

---

## Navigation

### 📬 [Cross-Posting Archive](cross-posting/)
Links to AI-KR, WebML, Voice Agents, Trust, Verifiable Credentials, and other CG discussions relevant to PM-KR.

### 📊 [Weekly Summaries](weekly-summaries/)
Condensed reports of PM-KR activity, literature reviews, cross-CG interactions, and action items.

### 📚 [Literature & Prior Art](literature/)
Papers, articles, and references on declarative/procedural KR, mandala graph theory, verifiable credentials, and related topics.

### 🏛️ [Architecture Decisions](decisions/)
Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) documenting key PM-KR design choices and rationale.

## Contributing

**Format:** Markdown (GitHub-flavored)
**Style:** Condensed, factual, no AI verbosity (per Milton's guidance)
**Version Control:** All changes tracked via Git
**Transparency:** Public archive per W3C requirements

---

## Purpose

**Milton Ponson's vision** (from PM-KR mailing list, March 1, 2026):

> "Sometimes I wonder if a Wiki page combining elements, findings, discussions of multiple Community Groups could help. We are already cross-posting all the time. Would be an excellent idea for the PM-KR community group."
>
> "As a mathematician I would also like to point out the usefulness of creating weekly summary reports of reviews of new literature of interest, points brought up in posts, and links to work in other community groups, based on interaction and reflections triggered by cross-posts."
>
> "And these should be written in a condensed summary form, without all the verbal add-ons that AI chatbots typically generate."

**Paola Di Maio's guidance** (from AI-KR CG, March 1, 2026):

> "It would be great to receive occasional updates from the new CG with concrete updates that may be relevant to AI KR - just send us the executive summaries from time to time?"

This wiki implements both requests: cross-CG collaboration infrastructure + executive summary format.
