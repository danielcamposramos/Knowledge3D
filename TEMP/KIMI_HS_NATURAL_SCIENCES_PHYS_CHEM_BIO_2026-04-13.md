## 4. Saudades Taxonomy & Lexicon

**Definition:** Saudades flags mark surface_form entries where locale-specific terminology creates semantic divergence risk.

### 4.1 Flag Categories
- **[S1-Orthographic]**: Spelling variants (en: *sulfur* / en-UK: *sulphur*)
- **[S2-Terminological]**: Distinct terms for same concept (de: *Wechselspannung* / pt-BR: *corrente alternada* / ja: 交流電圧)

- **[S5-IUPAC]**: Systematic vs. traditional chemical names (ethanoic acid vs acetic acid)
