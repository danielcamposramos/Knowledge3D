**K3D Phase 7.A.2 — Cross-Curricular Glue Cluster (Unified Specification)**

## 1. Executive Architecture

The Cross-Cultural Glue Cluster serves as the **semantic ligament** of the K3D knowledge graph, operating at the HS (High School) World Level. Unlike standard lexical clusters that map word-to-meaning within a single language, this cluster treats **meta-linguistic concepts, institutional systems, and cultural patterns** as first-class meaning-stars with full semantic weight.

**Core Design Principle**: Every star in this cluster is a **hypernode**—bidirectionally symlinked to Language (9×), Literature, Philosophy, History, Geography, Mathematics (conversions), and Economics clusters. This enables the AI to perform "cultural translation" (e.g., understanding that Portuguese *saudade*, Welsh *hiraeth*, and Japanese *mono no aware* occupy adjacent regions in affective space while remaining semantically distinct).

**RPN Operators**:
- `^` : Extraction/Attribution (e.g., `longing melancholic ^` = "melancholic type of longing")
- `&` : Conjunction/Co-occurrence (e.g., `absence_present &` = simultaneous presence of absence and presence)
- `→` : Transformation/Causation
- `±` : Cultural variance marker
